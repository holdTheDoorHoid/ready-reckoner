# Research reports

Background research commissioned on 2026-09-25 to ground the design. Each report marks anything it
could not verify against a primary source as UNVERIFIED and labels expert judgement as such. They are
inputs to `docs/DESIGN.md`, `docs/RISK_MODEL.md`, `docs/DATA_SOURCES.md` and the content catalogue;
where a report and a later document disagree, the later document wins and should say why.

| Report | Question it answers |
| --- | --- |
| `prior-art-and-psychology.md` | What exists already, what gap this fills, and what behavioural science says about how to present risk and grow a plan |
| `data-sources.md` | Which public datasets give per-location hazard probability and consequence data, their licences, sizes and how to bundle them |
| `supply-standards.md` | The cited per-person quantities (water, calories, medication, sanitation, power) and cost benchmarks behind every number the app prints |
| `risk-model.md` | The quantitative model: hazards -> consequence buckets -> design-event durations -> budget allocation, with worked examples |
