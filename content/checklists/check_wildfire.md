## Use this when

A wildfire is near, officials tell you to get ready or leave, or smoke or flames are coming your way.

## Do first

1. **Leave right away** when officials tell you to.[^ready_gov_wildfires]
2. **Leave early** if you feel at risk, or need extra time with children, pets or someone who needs help, even without an order.[^calfire_go_evacuation][^usfa_wildfire_evacuation]
3. **Put your go-bag in the car.**[^calfire_go_evacuation]
4. **Wear an N95 mask** in smoke.[^ready_gov_wildfires]
5. **Trapped?** Call 911 and give your location. Turn on lights for rescuers.[^ready_gov_wildfires]

## Then

1. **If you have time,** close windows and doors, but leave them unlocked.[^calfire_go_evacuation]
2. **Turn off the gas** at the meter, and pilot lights.[^calfire_go_evacuation]
3. **Move things that burn** away from windows. Take down curtains.[^calfire_go_evacuation]
4. **Leave lights on** so firefighters can see the house.[^calfire_go_evacuation]
5. **Connect garden hoses** to outside taps. Shut off propane tanks.[^calfire_go_evacuation]
6. **Park facing out,** loaded, with the windows shut.[^calfire_go_evacuation]
7. **Trapped in a car?** Park away from brush. Close windows and vents. Lie on the floor under a wool blanket or coat.[^calfire_go_evacuation]
8. **Trapped on foot?** Lie face down in a ditch or on clear, flat ground. Cover your body.[^calfire_go_evacuation]
9. **Trapped at home?** Fill sinks and tubs with cold water. Keep doors and windows shut, not locked. Stay away from outside walls.[^calfire_go_evacuation]

## Leave or stay

- **Leave if** officials tell you to, or you feel at risk.[^ready_gov_wildfires][^calfire_go_evacuation] Go to {where_go}.{if:pets} Take your pets.[^ready_gov_pets]{/if} {ref:getting_out}
- **Stay if** fire blocks every way out. Call 911 and shelter at home.[^calfire_go_evacuation]

## Do not

- Do not leave sprinklers or water running.[^calfire_go_evacuation]
- Do not touch power lines.[^calfire_go_evacuation] Stay at least 35 feet from downed lines.[^redcross_power_outage]

## When it is over

- Go home only when officials say it is safe.[^ready_gov_wildfires]
- Watch for hot ash, embers, smoldering debris and downed lines.[^ready_gov_wildfires][^calfire_go_evacuation]
- Clean up in long sleeves, gloves, sturdy shoes and a respirator.[^ready_gov_wildfires]
- For the weeks ahead, see {ref:after}.[^ready_gov_recovering]
