## Use this when

A severe thunderstorm or high wind warning is issued, you hear thunder, or damaging wind or hail is coming.

## Do first

1. **Go inside** a sturdy building or a hard-topped car when you hear thunder.[^nws_lightning_safety]
2. **Go to your safe spot,** an inside room or basement: {shelter_home}.[^nws_high_wind_during] {if:pets}Take your pets if there is time.[^nws_severe_weather_during]{/if}
3. **Stay away from windows** and outside doors.[^nws_severe_weather_during][^nws_lightning_safety]
4. **Stay off** corded phones, plugged-in devices and running water.[^nws_lightning_safety][^ready_gov_thunderstorms_lightning]
5. **Follow the warnings** on the radio, TV or your phone.[^ready_gov_thunderstorms_lightning][^nws_high_wind_during]

## Then

1. **Caught outside?** Get to a sturdy building or a hard-topped car. Sheds and rain shelters are not safe.[^nws_severe_weather_during][^nws_lightning_safety]
2. **Driving?** Hold the wheel with both hands and slow down. Head for a sturdy building if you can.[^nws_high_wind_during]
3. **No building near?** Stop where trees and power lines are less likely to fall on the car.[^nws_high_wind_during]
4. **Wait 30 minutes** after the last thunder before going outside.[^nws_lightning_safety]
5. **Someone struck by lightning?** Call 911. They are safe to touch. Start CPR or use an AED if needed.[^nws_lightning_safety]

## Leave or stay

- **Stay if** you are in a sturdy building, until 30 minutes after the last thunder.[^nws_lightning_safety] {if:home:mobile_home}A mobile home is not one: move to a sturdy building before the wind picks up.[^nws_high_wind_during]{/if}
- **Go to** the nearest sturdy building if you are outside or in a shed.[^nws_severe_weather_during]

## Where and who

- **Safe spot at home:** {shelter_home}
- **Safe spot at work or school:** {shelter_work}
- **How you get alerts:** {alerts}

## Do not

- Do not shelter under a tree, in a shed or in a rain shelter.[^nws_severe_weather_during][^nws_lightning_safety]
- Do not lie on a garage floor.[^nws_lightning_safety]
- Do not go into big open rooms at work or school, such as a gym or cafeteria.[^nws_severe_weather_during]
- Do not drive through flooded roads.[^ready_gov_thunderstorms_lightning]
- Do not go near fallen power lines. They may be live.[^nws_high_wind_during]

## When it is over

- Watch for fallen power lines and trees. Report fallen lines to the power company: {electric_utility}.[^ready_gov_thunderstorms_lightning][^cdc_electrical_hazards]
- Photograph damage before you clean up, then call your insurer.[^ready_gov_recovering][^naic_home_insurance_guide] {ref:after}
- Sign up for your community's warnings.[^ready_gov_thunderstorms_lightning] {if:need:hearing}A weather radio can set off a strobe light or bed shaker for anyone who cannot hear alerts.[^nws_nwr_deaf]{/if}
