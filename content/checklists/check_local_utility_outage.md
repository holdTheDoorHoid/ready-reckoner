## Use this when

No water comes from the tap, or a notice says not to drink or use it. Or the gas company shuts off gas to your home or street.

## Do first

1. **If you smell gas or hear hissing, get everyone out.** Call 911 and {gas_utility} from outside.[^ready_gov_safety_skills][^pa_puc_gas_emergencies]
2. **Read the water notice and follow it:** boil, do not drink, or do not use.[^cdc_water_advisories]
3. **Drink stored or bottled water.**[^ready_gov_water]
4. **Clean hands with sanitizer** that has at least 60% alcohol.[^cdc_hygiene_emergency]
5. **Set up a two-bucket toilet** if toilets cannot flush.[^rdpo_emergency_toilet]

## Then

1. **Heat safely.** Use electric space heaters with automatic shut-off, 3 feet from anything that can burn.[^cdc_winter_safety][^usfa_heating_fires]
2. {if:need:dialysis}**If your dialysis center is closed,** go to the other one you know.[^ready_gov_disability]{/if}

## Leave or stay

- **Leave if** you smell gas or hear hissing. Go outside and away from the building.[^ready_gov_safety_skills]
- **Leave if** you cannot keep the home warm. Go to a warming center (dial 2-1-1 to find one), or {where_go}.[^ready_gov_stay_safe_warm] {ref:getting_out}

## Where and who

- Water company: {water_utility}
- Gas company: {gas_utility}
- Water shut-off: {water_shutoff}

## Do not

- Do not turn the gas back on yourself. Only a qualified professional does that.[^ready_gov_safety_skills]
- If you smell gas, do not switch lights on or off or use a phone inside.[^pa_puc_gas_emergencies]
- Do not use water or ice from the fridge dispenser while a notice is on.[^cdc_water_advisories]
- Do not boil water under a do-not-drink notice. Boiling does not remove chemicals.[^cdc_water_advisories]

## When it is over

- Let the gas company or another qualified professional turn the gas back on.[^ready_gov_safety_skills][^redcross_power_outage]
- Refill your stored water.[^cdc_water_storage]
