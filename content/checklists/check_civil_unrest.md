## Use this when

There are riots, violent protests or a curfew near your home, your work or your route.

## Do first

1. **Stay away from the area.** Take another route.[^fairfax_civil_disorder]
2. **Go inside and lock the doors.** Stay away from windows and doors.[^fairfax_civil_disorder]
3. **Follow police directions** and any curfew.[^fairfax_civil_disorder]
4. **Stay informed** through official alerts or a battery radio.[^fairfax_civil_disorder]
5. **Text your household** to say where you are.[^fcc_emergency_calling_tips]

## Then

1. **Know your exit routes** in case you have to leave.[^fairfax_civil_disorder]
2. **Use your home supplies** of food, water and medicine.[^ready_gov_kit]
3. **Look out for your neighbors,** and ask them to look out for you.[^ncpc_home_safety]
4. **If someone near you is tense,** stay calm and friendly, and get help if you need it.[^cisa_deescalation]
5. **If violence starts near you, get away.** If you cannot, hide out of sight behind something solid.[^ready_gov_public_spaces]

## Leave or stay

- **Stay if** the trouble is not at your home. Stay inside, away from windows, in {shelter_home}.[^fairfax_civil_disorder] {ref:home}
- **Leave if** officials tell you to, or the danger reaches your street. Use your exit route to {where_go}.[^ready_gov_evacuation][^fairfax_civil_disorder] {ref:getting_out}
- **Call 911 if** you see the situation change near you, or someone is hurt.[^fairfax_civil_disorder]

## Where and who

- Shelter spot at home: {shelter_home}
- How we get alerts: {alerts}

## Do not

- Do not go to look. Stay away from the area.[^fairfax_civil_disorder]
- Do not stop for belongings if you have to get away.[^ready_gov_public_spaces]

## When it is over

- Check that the area is safe before you go out.[^fairfax_civil_disorder]
- Get first aid, or support for stress, if anyone needs it.[^fairfax_civil_disorder]
- Report any damage to your property to the police, and take photos for your insurer.[^fairfax_civil_disorder][^ready_gov_recovering] {ref:after}
