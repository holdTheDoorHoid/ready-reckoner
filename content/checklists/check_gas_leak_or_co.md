## Use this when

You smell gas or hear a blowing or hissing noise, or a carbon monoxide (CO) alarm sounds. Headache, dizziness, weakness, upset stomach or confusion can also mean CO.[^cdc_co_basics]

## Do first

1. **Get everyone out now.** Do not stop to look for the source.[^pa_puc_gas_emergencies][^cpsc_co_questions_answers]
2. **Leave windows and doors open** as you go.[^pa_puc_gas_emergencies][^ready_gov_safety_skills]
3. **Make no sparks.** Leave light switches as they are, and do not use a phone inside.[^pa_puc_gas_emergencies]
4. **Call 911 from outside,** away from any smell. For gas, call the gas company too.[^pa_puc_gas_emergencies][^cpsc_co_questions_answers]
5. **Count heads** once you are outside.[^cpsc_co_questions_answers]
6. **Stay out** until responders say it is safe to go back in.[^cpsc_co_questions_answers][^pa_puc_gas_emergencies]

## Then

1. **Turn off the gas** at the outside main valve if you can: {gas_shutoff}.[^ready_gov_safety_skills]
2. **Anyone sick?** Keep them in fresh air, and describe their symptoms to the call-taker.[^cpsc_co_questions_answers][^gov911_faq]
3. **Alarm sounds again** within a day of going back? Get out, call 911 again, then have a qualified technician check every fuel-burning appliance.[^cpsc_co_questions_answers]
4. **Keep a faulty appliance off** until trained workers have serviced it.[^cpsc_co_questions_answers]

## Leave or stay

- **Leave if** you smell gas, hear hissing or a CO alarm sounds. Go far enough that you cannot smell gas.[^pa_puc_gas_emergencies] {ref:getting_out}
- **Go to** your meeting place, {meeting_near}, and count heads.[^ready_gov_plan][^cpsc_co_questions_answers]
- **Call** 911 and the gas company, {gas_utility}, from outside.[^pa_puc_gas_emergencies]

## Where and who

- Gas company: {gas_utility}
- Gas shut-off: {gas_shutoff}

## Do not

- Do not turn lights or switches on or off, or use a phone, inside.[^pa_puc_gas_emergencies]
- Do not look for the leak or the source of the CO.[^cpsc_co_questions_answers]
- Never turn the gas back on yourself. Only a qualified professional does that.[^ready_gov_safety_skills]
- Never run a generator, grill or camp stove inside the home or garage, even with doors and windows open.[^cdc_co_basics]

## When it is over

- Put CO alarms with battery backup on every level and outside sleeping areas.[^cpsc_co_questions_answers]
- Ask the gas company how to shut off your meter. Do not turn it off to practice.[^ready_gov_safety_skills] {ref:home}
- Have your chimney checked or cleaned every year.[^cdc_co_basics]
- {if:has:power_generator}Run the generator only outdoors, more than 20 feet from windows, doors and vents.[^cdc_co_basics]{/if}
