## Use this when

A winter storm watch or warning covers your area.[^ready_gov_winter]

## Do first

1. **Stay home.** Stay off the roads if you can.[^ready_gov_winter]
2. **Stock up.** Have several days of food, water and medicine.[^ready_gov_winter]
3. **Lights.** Have a flashlight for each person.[^ready_gov_power_outages] {if:powered_device}Charge any medical device, and follow its power plan.[^ready_gov_power_outages]{/if}
4. **Protect pipes.** Let taps drip. Open the cabinets under sinks.[^cdc_winter_safety]
5. **Heat safely.** Never heat with a gas stove or oven.[^ready_gov_winter]
6. **Check on others.** Check on older neighbors often.[^cdc_winter_safety]

## Then

1. **One warm room.** If the heat fails, close off unused rooms. Stuff towels under doors.[^cdc_winter_safety] {if:children}Never let a baby sleep in a cold room.[^cdc_winter_safety]{/if}
2. **Dress in layers.** Wear loose, warm layers.[^nws_winter_storm_during]
3. **Space heaters.** Keep them 3 feet from anything that can burn.[^cdc_winter_safety]
4. **If you have to drive,** tell someone your route and when you will arrive.[^cdc_winter_safety] {if:vehicle}If the car gets stuck, stay in it. Run the engine 10 minutes an hour, with a window cracked and the exhaust clear of snow.[^nws_winter_storm_during]{/if}
5. **Shovel slowly.** Take breaks.[^ready_gov_winter]
6. **Cold injury.** Numb, white or waxy skin can be frostbite. Shivering, confusion or slurred speech can be hypothermia. Get the person warm.[^ready_gov_winter]

## Leave or stay

- **Stay if** you can keep one room warm.[^cdc_winter_safety] {ref:home}
- **Leave if** the home gets too cold. Go to a warming center; dial 2-1-1 to find one.[^ready_gov_stay_safe_warm] {ref:getting_out}
- **Call** 911 if a person's body temperature is below 95°F.[^ready_gov_winter]

## Do not

- Never run a generator, grill or camp stove inside the home or garage.[^cdc_co_basics][^cdc_winter_safety]
- Do not use candles. Use flashlights.[^cdc_winter_safety]
- Do not drive on icy roads, bridges or overpasses.[^cdc_winter_safety]
- Do not thaw pipes with a torch.[^cdc_winter_safety]
- Do not rub frostbite or warm it with a heating pad.[^ready_gov_winter]

## When it is over

- **Clear the furnace vent** of snow.[^nws_winter_storm_during]
- **Salt or sand** icy steps and walks.[^cdc_winter_safety]
- **If a pipe bursts,** shut off the water at {water_shutoff}.[^nws_aly_cold_safety]
- **Check food.** Throw out food that sat at 40°F or warmer for two hours.[^ready_gov_power_outages]
- **Check on neighbors** again.[^cdc_winter_safety]
