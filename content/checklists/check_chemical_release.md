## Use this when

Officials warn of a chemical spill or leak near you, or you see one happen. Some chemicals have no smell.[^ready_gov_chemical]

## Do first

1. **Listen** to local radio, TV or alerts, and follow the instructions.[^ready_gov_chemical]{if:need:hearing} Keep a phone on and close by. Emergency alerts come with a vibration.[^fcc_wea]{/if}
2. **Told to leave?** Go right away.[^ready_gov_chemical]
3. **Outside?** Move away upwind, then get into the nearest building.[^ready_gov_chemical]
4. **In a car?** Close the windows and vents. Turn off the heater and air conditioner.[^ready_gov_chemical]
5. **Told to stay inside?** Bring people and pets in. Close and lock windows and doors.[^ready_gov_chemical]
6. **Turn off** fans, heating and air conditioning.[^ready_gov_chemical]

## Then

1. **Go to an inside room** with as few windows and doors as possible.[^ready_gov_chemical]
2. **Close vents, fireplace dampers** and as many inside doors as you can.[^ready_gov_chemical]
3. **Seal the room** with plastic sheeting and duct tape. Stuff gaps with wet towels.[^ready_gov_chemical]
4. **Fumes got in?** Take shallow breaths through a cloth or towel.[^ready_gov_chemical]
5. **Chemical inside your building?** Get out without passing through it.[^ready_gov_chemical]
6. **Chemical on you?** Take off your clothes and seal them in a bag. Cut off anything that pulls over your head.[^ready_gov_chemical]
7. **Wash** your hands, face and hair with soap and water. Flush your eyes with water.[^ready_gov_chemical]
8. **Get checked** at a medical facility as soon as it is safe.[^ready_gov_chemical]

## Leave or stay

- **Leave if** officials tell you to.[^ready_gov_chemical] Go to {where_go}. {ref:getting_out}
- **Stay if** you are told to stay inside, or you cannot reach clean air without passing through the chemical. Seal your room.[^ready_gov_chemical] {ref:home}

## Do not

- Do not walk toward the spill or the cloud.[^ready_gov_chemical]
- Do not leave your shelter, even to help others, until officials say it is safe.[^ready_gov_chemical]
- Do not eat or drink food or water that may be contaminated.[^ready_gov_chemical]
- Do not drink tap water if officials say it is unsafe. Boiling does not remove chemicals.[^cdc_water_advisories]

## When it is over

- Go home only when officials say it is safe.[^ready_gov_chemical]
- For the weeks ahead, see {ref:after}.[^ready_gov_recovering]
