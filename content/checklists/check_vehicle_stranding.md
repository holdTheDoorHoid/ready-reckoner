## Use this when

Your vehicle breaks down or is stuck in snow, rising water or extreme heat.

## Do first

1. **Pull out of the traffic lanes** if you can. Turn on the hazard lights. Raise the hood if it is not snowing.[^adot_severe_weather][^cdc_winter_safety]
2. **Call for help** right away. Call 911 if anyone is hurt or in danger.[^adot_severe_weather]
3. **In fast-moving water, stay inside.** Get on the roof if water rises inside the car.[^ready_gov_floods]
4. **In a snowstorm, stay with the car.** Tie a bright cloth to the antenna.[^cdc_winter_safety]
5. **Run the engine** about 10 minutes an hour, a window cracked and the exhaust pipe clear of snow.[^cdc_winter_safety]
6. **In heat, drink water.** Run the air conditioning, or roll down all the windows.[^adot_severe_weather]

## Then

1. **Cold?** Move what you need from the trunk into the car. Wrap up, head included.[^cdc_winter_safety] {if:has:gethome_car_kit}Use the blankets and warm clothes in the car kit.[^ready_gov_winter]{/if}
2. **Stay awake** and keep moving your arms and legs. Huddle together for warmth.[^cdc_winter_safety]
3. **Out of the car in heat?** Keep shoes on, and carry pets off the hot pavement.[^adot_severe_weather]
4. **Power line on the car?** Stay inside unless the car is on fire. Warn others not to touch it.[^osha_downed_wires][^cdc_electrical_hazards]

## Leave or stay

- **Stay if** snow or ice makes it hard to see or drive.[^cdc_winter_safety]
- **Stay if** your car is caught in fast-moving water.[^ready_gov_floods]
- **Leave if** the car gets too hot inside. Take everyone, pets too, into shade away from traffic.[^adot_severe_weather]

- **Out-of-area contact:** {out_of_area_contact}

## Do not

- Do not drive or walk into floodwater. A foot of moving water can sweep a car away.[^ready_gov_floods]
- Do not stand in the traffic lanes.[^adot_severe_weather]
- Do not eat snow. It lowers your body temperature.[^cdc_winter_safety]
- Do not touch a fallen power line.[^cdc_electrical_hazards]
- Do not try to repair the car yourself in extreme heat.[^adot_severe_weather]

## When it is over

- Watch for hypothermia: shivering, confusion, slurred speech, drowsiness. Below 95°F, get medical help right away.[^cdc_winter_safety]
- Heavy sweating, nausea or fainting in heat? Get to a cool place, sip water and use cool wet cloths.[^cdc_hot_weather_psa]
- Restock the car kit and keep the gas tank at least half full.[^ready_gov_winter][^ready_gov_evacuation]
