## Use this when

Card payments, a bank, a pharmacy, a hospital or a utility stops working because of a cyberattack or a computer failure. Or you learn that your own information was stolen.

## Do first

1. **Pay with cash** in small bills.[^ready_gov_financial]
2. **Check your bank and card statements** for charges you did not make.[^ready_gov_cybersecurity]
3. **Tell your bank or card company** about any charge you did not make.[^ready_gov_cybersecurity]
4. **Change your passwords** if you see strange activity.[^ready_gov_cybersecurity]
5. **Try another pharmacy** if yours cannot fill a prescription.[^fda_drug_shortages_faq]
6. **Take your written medicine list** to the pharmacy or doctor.[^ready_gov_disability] {ref:people}

## Then

1. **Check your credit reports** for accounts or loans you did not open.[^ready_gov_cybersecurity]
2. **Turn on two-step sign-in** for your email and bank.[^ready_gov_cybersecurity]
3. **Install software updates.**[^cisa_secure_our_world]
4. **Tell your work or school** if a device you use there was affected.[^ready_gov_cybersecurity]
5. **Report identity theft** to the Federal Trade Commission.[^ready_gov_cybersecurity]
6. **Report online crime** to the FBI's Internet Crime Complaint Center (IC3).[^ready_gov_cybersecurity]
7. **File a police report** if you were a victim, so there is an official record.[^ready_gov_cybersecurity]

## Leave or stay

- **Do this if** your Social Security number was stolen: call Social Security at 1-800-269-0271.[^ready_gov_cybersecurity]
- **Do this if** someone who says they are from the government asks you for money: hang up. The government does not call, text or message you about owing money.[^ready_gov_cybersecurity]

## Do not

- Do not click links in texts or emails from people you do not know.[^ready_gov_cybersecurity]
- Do not give a caller control of your computer. Companies do not call to fix it.[^ready_gov_cybersecurity]
- Do not share PINs or passwords.[^ready_gov_cybersecurity]

## When it is over

- Keep checking your statements and credit reports.[^ready_gov_cybersecurity]
- Back up your files in encrypted storage.[^ready_gov_cybersecurity]
- Keep some cash at home in small bills.[^ready_gov_financial]
