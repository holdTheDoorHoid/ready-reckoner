## Use this when

Someone whose income the household relies on dies, or becomes unable to work for a long time.

## Do first

1. **Get several certified copies of the death certificate.**[^usagov_report_death]
2. **Call Social Security at 1-800-772-1213** to ask about survivor benefits.[^ssa_when_someone_dies]
3. **Tell banks, card companies and credit bureaus** about the death.[^usagov_report_death]
4. **Apply for Social Security disability** if the earner's condition will last a year or more and keeps them from working. Apply online or at 1-800-772-1213.[^ssa_apply_disability]
5. **Check your health coverage.** If you lose it, you can pick a Marketplace plan within 60 days.[^healthcare_gov_sep]
6. **Ask your trusted circle for help** with children, meals and errands.[^ollam_2022_lawyer_passport_locksmith_gun] {ref:contacts}

## Then

1. **Tell the other programs and businesses the person used,** and close or transfer accounts.[^usagov_report_death]
2. **If the earner is alive but cannot act,** ask the person named in their power of attorney to handle money and health care.[^nia_affairs_checklist]
3. **Get legal help** with the will and accounts. Legal aid helps people with low incomes.[^lsc_get_legal_help]
4. **Call or text 988** for support when grief or stress is too much.[^samhsa_988]

## Leave or stay

- **Do this if** rent or the mortgage will be late: call 211 or your local government about help.[^cfpb_rent_help]
- **Do this if** the person left debts: read the FTC's advice on what to do about them.[^usagov_report_death]

## Do not

- Do not take a payday loan. A typical two-week loan costs as much as almost 400 percent a year.[^cfpb_payday_loans]

## When it is over

- Update your own will and powers of attorney.[^nia_affairs_checklist]
