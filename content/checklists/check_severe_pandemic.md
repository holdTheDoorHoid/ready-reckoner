## Use this when

Officials say a pandemic is severe. Schools and child care may close, and you may be asked to stay home.[^cdc_mmwr_community_mitigation_2017]

## Do first

1. **Stay home.** Go out only for needs like food, medicine and medical care.[^ready_gov_shelter]
2. **One sick, all stay home.** If anyone at home is sick, everyone in the home stays home, as officials advise.[^cdc_mmwr_community_mitigation_2017]
3. **Keep the sick apart.** The sick person stays away from others at home, in their own room if you can.[^cdc_respiratory_when_sick]
4. **Masks at home.** The sick person wears a well-fitted mask around others.[^cdc_respiratory_when_sick][^cdc_mmwr_community_mitigation_2017]
5. **Wash hands** often, for at least 20 seconds.[^ready_gov_pandemic]
6. **Follow health officials.** Follow CDC and your health department.[^ready_gov_pandemic]

## Then

1. **Clean** surfaces people touch often.[^ready_gov_pandemic]
2. **Use your supplies.** Use stored food, water and medicines. Buy a little at a time.[^ready_gov_pandemic]
3. **Medicines.** Ask your doctor or pharmacist about an emergency supply.[^ready_gov_disability]
4. **School and work at home.** Plan for closed schools and for working from home.[^ready_gov_pandemic]
5. **Keep in touch** by phone and video calls.[^ready_gov_shelter]
6. **Look after your mind.** Feeling anxious is normal. Talk to someone, or call or text 988.[^ready_gov_pandemic][^samhsa_988]

## Leave or stay

- **Stay home** unless you need medical care or supplies.[^ready_gov_shelter] {ref:home}
- **Call** your doctor if someone is sick or was exposed. Follow their advice.[^ready_gov_pandemic]
- **Call** 911 for trouble breathing or chest pain. Wear a mask while you wait.[^cdc_respiratory_when_sick][^ready_gov_pandemic]

## Do not

- Do not have visitors if you can avoid it.[^ready_gov_shelter]
- Do not go out when sick, except for medical care.[^ready_gov_pandemic]
- Do not pass on rumors or unproven treatments.[^ready_gov_pandemic]
- Do not buy everything at once.[^ready_gov_pandemic]

## When it is over

- **Follow officials** on when schools and work reopen.[^cdc_mmwr_community_mitigation_2017]
- **Keep** washing hands and staying home when sick.[^ready_gov_pandemic]
- **Restock slowly,** and update your plan.[^ready_gov_pandemic]
