## Use this when

Someone at home will be sick or recovering for weeks or months, and needs care.

## Do first

1. **Ask your employer about family and medical leave.** Eligible workers can take up to 12 weeks of unpaid, job-protected leave to care for a spouse, child or parent.[^dol_fmla]
2. **Write down every medicine,** its dose, and any allergies.[^ready_gov_disability] {ref:people}
3. **Ask the doctor or pharmacist** how to keep an emergency supply of medicine.[^ready_gov_disability]
4. **Apply for Social Security disability** if an earner's illness will last a year or more and keeps them from working.[^ssa_apply_disability]
5. **Ask your trusted circle for help** with rides, meals and errands.[^ollam_2022_lawyer_passport_locksmith_gun] {ref:contacts}
6. {if:powered_device}**Make a power outage plan** for their medical equipment with their provider.[^ready_gov_power_outages]{/if}

## Then

1. **Help them name a health care proxy** in a durable power of attorney for health care.[^nia_affairs_checklist]
2. **Give someone else a key,** and show them where supplies are and how to use the equipment.[^ready_gov_older_adults]
3. **Look after the caregiver.** Keep regular sleep, and talk with people you trust.[^cdc_managing_stress]
4. **Call or text 988** if stress becomes too much.[^samhsa_988]

## Leave or stay

- **Do this if** you need more leave than federal law gives: ask your state labor department.[^dol_fmla]
- **Do this if** the household loses health coverage: pick a Marketplace plan within 60 days.[^healthcare_gov_sep]

## Where and who

- Pharmacy: {pharmacy}
- Nearest hospital: {hospital}

## Do not

- Do not share antibiotics or save them for later.[^cdc_antibiotic_use]
- Do not take a payday loan to cover bills. A typical two-week loan costs as much as almost 400 percent a year.[^cfpb_payday_loans]

## When it is over

- Update the medicine list and refill the emergency supply.[^ready_gov_disability]
- Keep the power of attorney papers with your other documents.[^nia_affairs_checklist] {ref:documents}
