## Use this when

Officials warn that a severe solar storm could cause wide power outages, or power and phones fail across a region. GPS and satellite service can fail too.[^ready_gov_space_weather]

## Do first

1. **Follow alerts.** Follow the instructions in emergency alerts.[^ready_gov_space_weather]
2. **Charge up.** Charge phones, power banks and laptops. Keep a phone charger in the car.[^ready_gov_space_weather] {if:powered_device}Charge any medical device, and follow its power plan.[^ready_gov_power_outages]{/if}
3. **Use less power.** Keep your electricity use low, to help avoid rolling blackouts.[^ready_gov_space_weather]
4. **Lights.** Have a flashlight for each person.[^ready_gov_power_outages]
5. **Cash.** Have some cash in small bills.[^ready_gov_financial] {if:vehicle}Keep the car's tank at least half full, since gas pumps need power.[^ready_gov_space_weather]{/if}
6. **Water.** Have one gallon per person per day stored, for several days.[^ready_gov_water]

## Then

1. **Unplug** appliances if officials tell you to.[^ready_gov_space_weather]
2. **Keep phone lines free.** Use the phone only when you need to.[^ready_gov_space_weather]
3. **Keep food cold.** Keep the fridge and freezer shut.[^ready_gov_power_outages]
4. **Cold medicines.** Keep them in the closed fridge. Ask your pharmacist how long each keeps without power.[^ready_gov_space_weather]
5. **Garage door.** Learn how to use the manual release on an electric opener.[^ready_gov_space_weather]
6. **Radio.** Use a battery or hand-crank radio for news.[^ready_gov_kit]

## Leave or stay

- **Stay if** your home is safe and not too hot or too cold.[^ready_gov_power_outages] {ref:home}
- **Go to** a community place with power if heat or cold becomes extreme.[^ready_gov_power_outages] {ref:neighbourhood}

## Where and who

## Do not

- Never run a generator inside the home or garage.[^cdc_co_basics]
- Do not plug a generator into a wall outlet.[^cpsc_generator_alert_2021]
- Do not heat the home with a gas stove or oven.[^ready_gov_power_outages]
- Do not taste food to see if it is safe.[^ready_gov_space_weather]

## When it is over

- **Check food.** Throw out food that sat at 40°F or warmer for two hours, or that smells or looks odd.[^ready_gov_space_weather]
- **Refreeze** food only if it still has ice crystals or is below 40°F.[^ready_gov_space_weather]
- **Ask your pharmacist** about any medicine that got warm.[^ready_gov_space_weather]
