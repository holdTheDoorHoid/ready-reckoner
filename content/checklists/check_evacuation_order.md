## Use this when

Officials order your area to leave, for any reason.

## Do first

1. **Go right away.** Leave as soon as officials tell you to.[^ready_gov_chemical]
2. **Grab the go-bag.** Take phones, chargers, medicines, ID, cash and the car kit.[^cdc_evacuation_psa] {if:powered_device}Take medical devices and their chargers.[^ready_gov_disability]{/if} {if:pets}Take your pets and their supplies.[^ready_gov_evacuation]{/if}
3. **Tell your contact.** Tell {out_of_area_contact} where you are going.[^ready_gov_evacuation]
4. **Lock up.** Close and lock the doors and windows.[^ready_gov_evacuation]
5. **Follow the route.** Take the routes officials give.[^ready_gov_evacuation]
6. **Listen.** Keep a battery radio on for instructions.[^ready_gov_evacuation] {if:need:hearing}Use a weather radio with a flashing alert and text.[^ready_gov_disability]{/if}

## Then

1. **Unplug** small appliances. Leave the fridge plugged in unless flooding is likely.[^ready_gov_evacuation]
2. **Shut off utilities** only if told to: water at {water_shutoff}, gas at {gas_shutoff}, power at {electric_panel}.[^ready_gov_evacuation]
3. **Leave a note** saying when you left and where you are going.[^ready_gov_evacuation]
4. **Dress for it.** Wear sturdy shoes, long pants, long sleeves and a hat.[^ready_gov_evacuation]
5. **Check on others.** Offer a ride to neighbors who need one.[^ready_gov_evacuation] {if:children}Follow official directions on where to pick up children from school.[^fema_back_to_school_2026]{/if}

## Leave or stay

- **Go to** {where_go}. If you are split up, meet at {meeting_far}.[^ready_gov_evacuation] {ref:getting_out}
- **Go to** a public shelter if needed. Text SHELTER and your ZIP code to 43362.[^ready_gov_shelter] {if:pets}Many do not allow pets.[^ready_gov_shelter]{/if}
- **Call** the {county} emergency management office if you have no way to leave.[^ready_gov_evacuation]

## Do not

- Do not take shortcuts. They may be blocked.[^ready_gov_evacuation]
- Do not drive into flooded areas. Watch for washed-out roads and downed power lines.[^ready_gov_evacuation]
- Do not go home until officials say it is safe.[^ready_gov_evacuation]

## When it is over

- **Check** with officials, where you are and back home, before you travel.[^ready_gov_evacuation]
- **Tell** family and friends when you leave and when you arrive.[^ready_gov_evacuation]
- **Fill up and charge up.** Fill the tank, charge devices, and bring water and food.[^ready_gov_evacuation]
- **Stay away** from downed lines, and report them to {electric_utility}.[^ready_gov_evacuation]
- **Run a generator** only outside, away from the home. Never connect it to the home's wiring.[^ready_gov_evacuation] {ref:after}
