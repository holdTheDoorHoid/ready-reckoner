## Use this when

A smoke alarm sounds, or you see or smell smoke or fire in your home.

## Do first

1. **Get out.** Leave by the nearest safe way; do not stop for anything.[^usfa_home_fire_escape_plans]
2. {if:home:apartment_low_rise|apartment_high_rise}**In an apartment building,** close your door behind you, pull the fire alarm on the way out and take the stairs, not the elevator.[^usfa_home_fire_escape_plans]{/if}
3. **Stay low.** If there is smoke, crawl under it to your way out.[^ready_gov_home_fires]
4. **Feel doors first.** If a door or its knob is hot, keep it closed and use your second way out.[^ready_gov_home_fires]
5. **Meet outside.** Go to your meeting place and stay there.[^usfa_home_fire_escape_plans]
6. **Call 911 from outside.** Call once you are out, not before.[^usfa_home_fire_escape_plans]

## Then

1. **Count heads.** Check that everyone is at the meeting place.[^usfa_home_fire_escape_plans]
2. **Tell firefighters.** If a person or pet is still inside, tell firefighters right away.[^ready_gov_home_fires]
3. **If you are trapped, seal the room.** Close the door and cover vents and cracks with cloth or tape.[^ready_gov_home_fires]
4. **Signal for help.** Call 911 and wave a light-colored cloth or a flashlight at the window.[^ready_gov_home_fires]
5. **If your clothes catch fire, stop, drop and roll.**[^ready_gov_home_fires]

## Leave or stay

- **Leave if** there is fire or smoke in the home. Go to {meeting_near}. {ref:neighbourhood}
- **Stay in a room only if** every way out is blocked. Seal the door and signal from the window.
- **Go to** {where_go} if the home cannot be lived in. {ref:getting_out}

## Do not

- Do not go back inside for anything, even a pet.[^usfa_home_fire_escape_plans]
- Do not open a door that is hot or has smoke coming around it.[^ready_gov_home_fires]
- Do not hide from firefighters.[^ready_gov_home_fires]

## When it is over

- Go back inside only when the fire department says it is safe.[^ready_gov_home_fires]
- Call your landlord or insurer, and list what was lost.[^ready_gov_home_fires]
- Put in new smoke alarms and test them every month.[^usfa_smoke_alarms]
- {if:need:hearing}For anyone deaf or hard of hearing, use smoke alarms with a vibrating pad, flashing light or strobe light.[^usfa_fire_safety_disabilities]{/if}
