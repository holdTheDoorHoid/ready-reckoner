## Use this when

The United States is at war, and power, water, fuel, phone or internet systems are attacked or shut down. At home, this mostly means outages.[^cisa_volt_typhoon_2024]

## Do first

1. **Follow official instructions.**[^ready_gov_evacuation]
2. **Turn on emergency alerts** on every phone.[^ready_gov_alerts]
3. **Keep a battery or hand-crank radio** on for news.[^ready_gov_kit] {if:need:hearing}If someone cannot hear it, watch phone alerts and texts.[^fcc_emergency_calling_tips]{/if}
4. **Fill clean bottles or jugs with tap water** while it runs.[^cdc_water_storage]
5. **Get some cash** in small bills.[^ready_gov_financial]
6. **Ask your drug plan** about a 60- to 90-day refill.[^medicare_drugs_disaster]

## Then

1. **Fill the car's tank.**[^ready_gov_evacuation]
2. **If the power goes out,** use flashlights, not candles, and keep the fridge closed.[^redcross_power_outage][^ready_gov_power_outages]
3. {if:powered_device}**Switch medical devices to backup power.** If it will not last, go where there is power.[^ready_gov_power_outages][^redcross_power_outage]{/if}
4. **Watch for scams.** Do not click links in texts or emails from people you do not know.[^ready_gov_cybersecurity]
5. **Install software updates.**[^cisa_secure_our_world]
6. **Report anything suspicious to local police:** who or what, when, where and why.[^dhs_report_suspicious]
7. **Keep important numbers on paper,** not only in your phone.[^ready_gov_low_cost]
8. **Check on your people and neighbours.**[^redcross_power_outage]
9. **Take breaks from the news.**[^cdc_managing_stress]
10. **Talk to someone** when stress builds. Call or text 1-800-985-5990.[^samhsa_disaster_distress]

## Leave or stay

- **Stay if** officials tell you to stay inside. Go to {shelter_home}.[^ready_gov_shelter] {ref:home}
- **Leave if** officials tell you to. Go to {where_go}.[^ready_gov_evacuation] {ref:getting_out}
- **Leave if** the home gets too hot or too cold, or a medical device needs power you cannot supply.[^redcross_power_outage] {ref:getting_out}

## Where and who

- Out-of-area contact: {out_of_area_contact}
- Local alerts: {alerts}

## Do not

- Never use a generator, camp stove or charcoal grill inside, even in the garage.[^cdc_co_basics][^ready_gov_power_outages]
- Do not share PINs or passwords.[^ready_gov_cybersecurity]
- Never heat your home with a gas stove or oven.[^ready_gov_power_outages]

## When it is over

- Refill your stored water.[^cdc_water_storage]
- Keep some cash at home in small bills.[^ready_gov_financial]
- If there was damage, take photos before you clean up.[^ready_gov_recovering] {ref:after}
