## Use this when

An extreme heat warning is out, or it will stay above 90°F for two or more days.[^ready_gov_heat]

## Do first

1. **Stay cool.** Stay in air conditioning. If you have none, go to a cooling center. Dial 2-1-1 to find one.[^ready_gov_heat][^cdc_heat_health]
2. **Drink.** Drink plenty of fluids.[^ready_gov_heat]
3. **Cars.** Never leave people or pets in a closed car.[^ready_gov_heat]
4. **Check on others.** Check on older adults, family and neighbors.[^ready_gov_heat]
5. **Know heat stroke.** Body temperature of 103°F or more, hot red skin that is dry or damp, confusion or passing out: call 911.[^cdc_heat_related_illness]
6. **Rest at midday.** Avoid hard work outside in the middle of the day.[^ready_gov_heat]

## Then

1. **Cool down.** Take cool showers or baths.[^ready_gov_heat]
2. **Dress light.** Wear loose, lightweight, light-colored clothes.[^ready_gov_heat]
3. **Block the sun.** Close drapes or shades. Use the oven less.[^ready_gov_heat]
4. **Outside,** stay in the shade and wear a wide hat.[^ready_gov_heat] {if:pets}Give pets shade and plenty of cool water.[^ready_gov_heat]{/if}
5. **Heat stroke care.** While you wait for help, move the person somewhere cooler. Cool them with cool cloths or a cool bath.[^cdc_heat_related_illness]
6. **Heat exhaustion.** Heavy sweating, weakness, dizziness or nausea: move somewhere cooler, take off extra clothes and sip water. Call a doctor if it gets worse or lasts over an hour.[^ready_gov_heat]
7. **If the power goes out** and the home gets too hot, go to a place with power.[^ready_gov_heat][^ready_gov_power_outages] {if:powered_device}Use the backup plan for any medical device.[^ready_gov_power_outages]{/if} {if:has:med_cooler_refrigerated_rx}Keep insulin below 86°F and out of the sun.[^fda_insulin_emergency]{/if}

## Leave or stay

- **Stay if** your air conditioning works.[^ready_gov_heat] {ref:home}
- **Go to** a cooling center, library or mall if your home has no air conditioning.[^ready_gov_heat] {ref:neighbourhood}
- **Call** 911 for signs of heat stroke.[^cdc_heat_related_illness]

## Where and who

- Cooling center or shelter: {shelter}

## Do not

- Do not rely on a fan when it is over 90°F indoors. It can make you hotter.[^cdc_heat_health]
- Do not give a person with heat stroke anything to drink.[^cdc_heat_related_illness]

## When it is over

- **Check on** older neighbors and family.[^ready_gov_heat]
- **After a power cut,** throw out food that was 40°F or warmer for 2 hours or more.[^ready_gov_power_outages]
