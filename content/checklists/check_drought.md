## Use this when

Officials declare a drought, or set limits on water use.[^ready_gov_drought]

## Do first

1. **Follow the limits.** Follow every state and local rule on water use.[^ready_gov_drought]
2. **Fix leaks.** Fix dripping taps and other leaks.[^ready_gov_drought]
3. **Take short showers.** Take short showers instead of baths.[^ready_gov_drought]
4. **Turn off the tap** while you brush your teeth, wash your face or shave.[^ready_gov_drought]
5. **Run full loads.** Run the washer and dishwasher only when full.[^ready_gov_drought]

## Then

1. **Water outside less.** If the lawn needs water, water early in the morning or late in the evening.[^ready_gov_drought]
2. **Save the trees.** In extreme drought, let the lawn go and keep trees and large shrubs alive.[^ready_gov_drought]
3. **Reuse water.** Catch the water that runs while it warms up, and use it on plants.[^ready_gov_drought]
4. **Flush less.** Put tissues and other trash in the bin, not the toilet.[^ready_gov_drought]
5. **Wash food in a pan** of water, not under a running tap.[^ready_gov_drought]
6. **Sweep** driveways and walks instead of hosing them.[^ready_gov_drought]
7. **Check a well pump.** If it turns on when no water is being used, you have a leak.[^ready_gov_drought]
8. **Keep stored water.** Keep at least one gallon per person per day, for several days.[^ready_gov_water]

## Leave or stay

- **Call** {water_utility} for the current limits and for help.[^ready_gov_drought]

## Do not

- Do not leave sprinklers or hoses running unattended.[^ready_gov_drought]
- Do not pour water down the drain if you can use it for something else.[^ready_gov_drought]
- Do not thaw food under running water.[^ready_gov_drought]
- Do not rinse dishes before they go in the dishwasher. Just scrape them.[^ready_gov_drought]

## When it is over

- **Keep saving water.** Make it a daily habit.[^ready_gov_drought]
- **Check** pipes and sprinklers for leaks.[^ready_gov_drought]
