## Use this when

You or another earner in the household loses a job, or most of their hours.

## Do first

1. **File for unemployment** with your state as soon as you can.[^dol_unemployment_insurance]
2. **Choose health coverage within 60 days.** Ask about COBRA, or apply for a Marketplace plan, Medicaid or CHIP.[^healthcare_gov_sep][^healthcare_gov_unemployed]
3. **Gather your papers:** pay stubs, bank and insurance records, and the numbers of people to call.[^fema_effak] {ref:documents}

## Then

1. **Get food help** from the USDA National Hunger Hotline, 1-866-348-6479.[^usda_hunger_hotline]
2. **Apply for rent help early** if rent will be hard to pay. Call 211 or your local government. Help can take several weeks.[^cfpb_rent_help]

## Leave or stay

- **Do this if** you cannot afford a Marketplace plan: get low-cost care at a community health center.[^healthcare_gov_unemployed]
- **Do this if** a landlord files to evict you: file a written answer and go to court.[^cfpb_facing_eviction]

## Where and who

## Do not

- Do not take a payday loan. A typical two-week loan costs as much as almost 400 percent a year.[^cfpb_payday_loans]

## When it is over

- Report your new job and income to the Marketplace right away, if you have a plan there.[^healthcare_gov_unemployed]
- Rebuild your emergency fund. Save a little automatically each payday.[^cfpb_emergency_fund]
