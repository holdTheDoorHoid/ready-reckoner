## Use this when

A hole opens or the ground sinks near your home, or walls start to sink, sag or crack.

## Do first

1. **Keep people and pets back** from the hole. Keep children away.[^fl_dep_sinkhole_faq]
2. **Get out of the house** if its walls are sinking, sagging or cracking.[^fl_dep_sinkhole_faq]
3. **Call 911** if lives or the home are in danger.[^fl_dep_sinkhole_faq]
4. **Mark off the hole** with a fence, rope or tape.[^fl_dep_sinkhole_faq]

## Then

1. **Call your insurer** right away if the hole threatens your home.[^fl_dep_sinkhole_faq] {ref:home}
2. **Smell gas?** Leave, and call 911 and the gas company from far away: {gas_utility}.[^pa_puc_gas_emergencies]
3. **Hole in a street?** Mark it off, tell the police and call the city or county road department.[^fl_dep_sinkhole_faq]
4. **Stay back from the edges** for several days, longer after heavy rain.[^fl_dep_sinkhole_faq]
5. **Take photos** of the hole and any damage.[^ready_gov_recovering]
6. **Hole next door?** Check your own yard for soft or sinking spots.[^fl_dep_sinkhole_faq]

## Leave or stay

- **Leave if** the hole is affecting the house and its walls sink, sag or crack. Go to {where_go}.[^fl_dep_sinkhole_faq] {ref:getting_out}
- **Stay if** the hole is small and away from the house. Mark it off and keep people away.[^fl_dep_sinkhole_faq]
- **Call** 911 if lives or the home are in immediate danger.[^fl_dep_sinkhole_faq]

## Where and who

- Where we would go: {where_go}
- Gas company: {gas_utility}

## Do not

- Do not put water into the hole. It can make the hole bigger.[^fl_dep_sinkhole_faq]
- Never throw trash or anything that could pollute the groundwater into it.[^fl_dep_sinkhole_faq]
- Do not go back into a house with sinking, sagging or cracking walls.[^fl_dep_sinkhole_faq]

## When it is over

- Have a licensed geologist or engineer find the cause.[^fl_dep_sinkhole_faq]
- A small hole away from buildings can often be filled with clean sand or soil.[^fl_dep_sinkhole_faq]
- Keep notes and dates of every talk with your insurer.[^naic_home_insurance_guide] {ref:after}
