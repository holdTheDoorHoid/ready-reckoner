## Use this when

An avalanche warning covers your area or your roads, or snow slides while you are in the mountains, or someone is buried.

## Do first

1. **Caught in a slide?** Try to get off the moving snow.[^nac_perform_rescue]
2. **Fight to keep your head** above the snow.[^nac_perform_rescue]
3. **As it slows,** put your hands in front of your face.[^nac_perform_rescue]
4. **Someone buried?** Watch the spot where you last saw them, then call 911.[^nac_perform_rescue][^ready_gov_avalanche]
5. **Search only if it is safe** from another slide.[^ready_gov_avalanche][^nac_perform_rescue]
6. **Use your beacon, probe and shovel** to find them and dig them out.[^usfs_avalanche_safety]

## Then

1. **Treat for cold and injuries** if you are trained.[^ready_gov_avalanche][^nac_perform_rescue]
2. **Get anyone cold to a warm room or shelter.** Keep them dry and wrapped in blankets, head and neck too.[^ready_gov_avalanche]
3. **Warm the center of the body first:** chest, neck, head and groin.[^ready_gov_avalanche]
4. **Be ready to spend the night out** if help is far away.[^nac_perform_rescue]
5. **On the road,** follow closures and warnings.[^ready_gov_avalanche]

## Leave or stay

- **Leave if** you are on or below a steep slope while warnings are out, or you see recent slides or cracks running across the snow.[^ready_gov_avalanche]
- **Stay if** the road ahead is closed. Wait, and do not stop where signs say not to.[^ready_gov_avalanche]
- **Call** 911 first if someone is buried, then search if it is safe.[^ready_gov_avalanche]

## Do not

- Do not go into avalanche terrain alone.[^usfs_avalanche_safety]
- Do not search where another slide could reach you.[^nac_perform_rescue]
- Do not cross slopes steeper than 30 degrees, or the ground below them, when danger is high.[^ready_gov_avalanche]

## When it is over

- Watch for hypothermia: shivering, confusion, fumbling hands, slurred speech, drowsiness. Below 95°F is an emergency.[^ready_gov_avalanche]
- Feeling anxious or upset is normal. Talk to someone.[^ready_gov_avalanche]
- Take a certified avalanche course before you go out again.[^usfs_avalanche_safety]
- Sign up for alerts from a Forest Service avalanche center near you.[^ready_gov_avalanche]
