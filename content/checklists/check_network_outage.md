## Use this when

Calls will not go through, your phone shows no service, or the internet is down for more than a few minutes. In a large outage, calls to 911 can fail too.[^fcc_att_outage_2024]

## Do first

1. **Try another way.** Send a text or an email, or use a landline if you have one.[^fcc_emergency_calling_tips]
2. **Use Wi-Fi calling** if your internet still works.[^fcc_att_outage_2024]
3. **If 911 does not answer,** call the police or fire number on your family card.[^ready_gov_family_comm_card]
4. **Wait 10 seconds before you redial.**[^fcc_emergency_calling_tips]
5. **Use your paper list of numbers.**[^ready_gov_low_cost]
6. **Get news from a battery or hand-crank radio.**[^ready_gov_kit]

## Then

1. **Text your out-of-area contact** to say where you are.[^fcc_emergency_calling_tips]
2. **Keep calls short** and make only the ones you need.[^fcc_emergency_calling_tips]
3. {if:has:comms_frs_radios}**Use two-way radios** to reach family nearby.[^fcc_frs]{/if}
4. **Pay with cash** if card readers are down.[^ready_gov_financial]

## Leave or stay

- **Go to** {meeting_near} if you cannot reach your household.[^ready_gov_plan] {ref:neighbourhood}

## Where and who

- Meeting place near home: {meeting_near}
- Out-of-area contact: {out_of_area_contact}

## Do not

- Do not count on texting 911. It is not available everywhere, so call if you can.[^fcc_text_911]
- Do not make calls while driving. Stop first.[^fcc_emergency_calling_tips]
- Do not click links in texts or emails from people you do not know.[^ready_gov_cybersecurity]

## When it is over

- Tell your household and your out-of-area contact that you are OK.[^ready_gov_plan]
- Write your police and fire departments' regular numbers on your family card.[^ready_gov_family_comm_card]
- Turn on Wi-Fi calling on every phone before the next outage.[^fcc_att_outage_2024]
