## Use this when

Smoke is forecast, or AirNow or the local news says the air is unhealthy.[^epa_wildfire_indoor_air]

## Do first

1. **Stay inside.** Keep windows and doors closed.[^epa_wildfire_indoor_air]
2. **Recirculate.** Set the heating and cooling system to recirculate.[^epa_wildfire_indoor_air]
3. **Clean room.** Close off one room. Run an air cleaner there on high.[^epa_clean_room][^epa_wildfire_indoor_air]
4. **Check the air.** Follow AirNow or the local news.[^epa_wildfire_indoor_air]
5. **Go easy.** Avoid hard exercise.[^epa_wildfire_indoor_air]
6. **Mask to go out.** Wear a two-strap N95 that seals over the nose and under the chin.[^epa_protect_lungs_2026] {if:children}Respirators do not come in sizes for young children, so keep them in the clean room.[^epa_protect_lungs_2026]{/if}

## Then

1. **No air cleaner?** Tape a MERV 13 furnace filter to a box fan.[^epa_diy_air_cleaners][^epa_wildfire_indoor_air]
2. **Window air conditioner?** Close its outdoor air damper. If you cannot, turn it off.[^epa_wildfire_indoor_air]
3. **Heart or lung disease?** Follow your provider's plan or asthma action plan. Ask a doctor before wearing a respirator.[^epa_wildfire_indoor_air][^epa_protect_lungs_2026]
4. **Stock up.** Keep at least five days of food and medicine, so you can stay in.[^epa_wildfire_indoor_air]

## Leave or stay

- **Stay if** you can keep the air inside cleaner, and stay cool with fans or air conditioning.[^epa_wildfire_indoor_air] {ref:home}
- **Leave if** the home is too hot or smoky inside. Go to a friend's home out of the smoke, or a cleaner-air shelter.[^epa_clean_room] {ref:neighbourhood}
- **Leave if** officials tell you to because a fire is near.[^epa_wildfire_indoor_air] {ref:getting_out}

## Do not

- Do not fry or broil food, burn candles or incense, smoke, or use aerosol sprays.[^epa_wildfire_indoor_air]
- Do not use a wood, gas or propane stove.[^epa_wildfire_indoor_air]
- Do not vacuum unless the vacuum has a HEPA filter.[^epa_wildfire_indoor_air]
- Do not use an air cleaner that makes ozone.[^epa_clean_room]
- Do not count on masks with ear loops or one strap. They do not seal.[^epa_protect_lungs_2026]

## When it is over

- **Air out the home.** Open windows, and run the kitchen and bathroom fans.[^epa_wildfire_indoor_air]
- **Change** dirty filters in the air cleaner and the heating system.[^epa_wildfire_indoor_air]
- **Clean up ash** in gloves, long sleeves and an N95. Mist it with water and wet-mop; do not dry-sweep. Children, older adults and people with heart or lung disease should not help.[^epa_wildfire_indoor_air]
