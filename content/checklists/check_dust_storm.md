## Use this when

A dust storm warning is out, or a wall of dust is coming. Most dust storms pass in a few minutes.[^nws_dust_storms]

## Do first

1. **Driving? Pull off.** Pull off the pavement as far as you can, and stop.[^nws_dust_storms]
2. **Lights off.** Turn off all your lights. Set the parking brake, and take your foot off the brake pedal.[^nws_dust_storms]
3. **At home, stay in.** Go inside and close the windows.[^cdc_valley_fever_prevention]
4. **Close up.** Close the doors and air vents.[^ready_gov_shelter]
5. **Filter the air.** Run an air cleaner if you have one.[^cdc_valley_fever_prevention]

## Then

1. **Cannot pull off?** Slow down, turn on your lights and sound the horn now and then. Follow the painted center line, and look for a safe place to pull off.[^nws_dust_storms]
2. **Caught outside?** Wear a fitted N95 respirator if you cannot get out of the dust.[^cdc_valley_fever_prevention]

## Leave or stay

- **Stay if** you are indoors. Keep the windows closed until the dust passes.[^cdc_valley_fever_prevention] {ref:home}
- **Stay if** you are driving and cannot see. Stay pulled off the road with your lights off.[^nws_dust_storms]

## Where and who

## Do not

- Never stop in a lane of traffic.[^nws_dust_storms]
- Do not leave your lights on once you park. Drivers behind you may follow them and hit you.[^nws_dust_storms]
- Do not drive into a dust storm if you can avoid it.[^nws_dust_storms]

## When it is over

- **See a doctor** if a cough, fever or tiredness follows the dust. It can be Valley fever, and early treatment helps.[^cdc_valley_fever]
