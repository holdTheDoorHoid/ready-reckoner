## Use this when

Someone in your household is arrested, or held by the police or another agency.

## Do first

1. **Call your lawyer.**[^ollam_2022_lawyer_passport_locksmith_gun] {ref:contacts}
2. **Arrange care for any children and pets** with your trusted circle.[^ollam_2022_lawyer_passport_locksmith_gun] {ref:contacts}
3. **Answer calls from unknown numbers** until they are home.[^ollam_2022_lawyer_passport_locksmith_gun]

## Then

1. **If they cannot afford a lawyer,** they have the right to one the government provides.[^aclu_stopped_by_police]
2. **If they gave you power of attorney,** use it to handle their bills and health care while they cannot.[^nia_affairs_checklist]
3. **Call or text 988** if the stress is too much.[^samhsa_988]

## Leave or stay

- **Do this if** immigration officers (ICE) are holding them: search ICE's online detainee locator with their name, country of birth and birth date, or their A-number.[^usagov_ice_detainee]
- **Do this if** they were arrested at a protest: call your area's legal hotline, if one runs there.[^nlg_mass_defense]

## Where and who

## Do not

- Do not talk about what happened on calls from custody. Police can listen to them, except calls with a lawyer.[^aclu_stopped_by_police]

## When it is over

- Keep your lawyer's number on paper, and learn one or two numbers by heart.[^ollam_2022_lawyer_passport_locksmith_gun]
