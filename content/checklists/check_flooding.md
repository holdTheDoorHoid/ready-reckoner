## Use this when

A flood or flash flood warning covers your area, or water is rising around you. Flash floods can come without warning.[^ready_gov_floods]

## Do first

1. **Get to higher ground** right away if you are in a low area.[^nws_flood_during]
2. **Leave at once** if officials tell you to.[^ready_gov_floods]
3. **Stay out of floodwater.** Do not walk, swim or drive through it.[^ready_gov_floods]
4. **Turn around** at a flooded road or a barrier.[^ready_gov_floods]
5. **Stay off bridges** over fast-moving water.[^ready_gov_floods]
6. **Trapped in a building?** Go to the highest floor. Go on the roof only if needed, and signal for help.[^ready_gov_floods]

## Then

1. **Listen** to alerts, a weather radio or local news.[^ready_gov_floods]{if:need:hearing} Keep a phone on and close by. Emergency alerts come with a vibration.[^fcc_wea]{/if}
2. **Before you leave,** lock the house. If you have time, turn off utilities and unplug appliances.[^nws_flood_during]
3. **Stay out of any room** where water covers outlets or cords.[^nws_flood_during]
4. **Car caught in fast water?** Stay inside. Get on the roof if water comes in.[^ready_gov_floods]
5. **Trapped by moving water outside?** Get to the highest point you can and call 911.[^nws_flood_during]

## Leave or stay

- **Leave if** officials tell you to, or water starts to rise around your home.[^ready_gov_floods][^nws_flood_during] Go to {where_go}.{if:pets} Take your pets.[^ready_gov_pets]{/if} {ref:getting_out}
- **Stay if** the roads are flooded and your home is dry. Move to a higher floor.[^ready_gov_floods] {ref:home}
- **Call** 911 if you are trapped by rising water.[^nws_flood_during]

## Do not

- Do not drive around barriers. The road may be gone under the water.[^ready_gov_floods][^nws_flood_during]
- Do not climb into a closed attic.[^ready_gov_floods]
- Do not touch electrical equipment if it is wet or you are standing in water.[^ready_gov_floods]
- Do not wade in floodwater. It can hide debris and carry sewage or electric current.[^ready_gov_floods]

## When it is over

- Go home only when officials say it is safe.[^ready_gov_floods]
- Clean up in heavy gloves and boots. Watch for snakes.[^ready_gov_floods]
- Run a generator only outdoors, away from windows.[^ready_gov_floods]
- Keep children out of the clean-up.[^ready_gov_floods]
- For the weeks ahead, see {ref:after}.[^ready_gov_recovering]
