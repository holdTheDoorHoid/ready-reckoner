## Use this when

Heavy rain soaks steep or burned slopes, or you hear rumbling, trees cracking or boulders knocking. New cracks, doors that stick or tilting fences and poles can mean the ground is moving slowly.

## Do first

1. **Leave** if officials tell you to, or if you feel unsafe at home.[^ready_gov_landslides]
2. **In its path?** Move uphill as fast as you can.[^ready_gov_landslides]
3. **Stay awake and alert** during a storm that could cause a landslide.[^ready_gov_landslides]
4. **Listen for warnings** on a battery-powered radio.[^ready_gov_landslides]
5. **Watch streams** for water that suddenly rises, drops or turns muddy.[^ready_gov_landslides]
6. **Follow the instructions** of local emergency managers.[^ready_gov_landslides]

## Then

1. **Keep away from river valleys and low ground** while the danger lasts.[^ready_gov_landslides]
2. **Need a shelter?** Text SHELTER and your ZIP code to 43362.[^ready_gov_landslides]
3. **Someone hurt or trapped near the slide?** Call 911.[^fema_until_help_arrives] Guide rescuers to them without going into the slide area.[^ready_gov_landslides]

## Leave or stay

- **Leave if** you are told to go, or you feel unsafe in your home. Go to {where_go}.[^ready_gov_landslides] {ref:getting_out}
- **Leave if** you hear rumbling or see mud or rocks moving toward you. Move uphill, out of the path.[^ready_gov_landslides]

## Where and who

- Where we would go: {where_go}
- How we get alerts: {alerts}

## Do not

- Do not stay to see if a debris flow is coming. By the time you are sure, it is too late to get away safely.[^ready_gov_landslides]
- Do not cross a road with water or mud flowing on it.[^ready_gov_landslides]
- Do not cross a bridge if you see a flow coming.[^ready_gov_landslides]
- Do not go into the slide area, even to help someone. Show rescuers where to go.[^ready_gov_landslides]

## When it is over

- Stay away from the slide area. More slides can follow.[^ready_gov_landslides]
- Watch for flooding after a landslide.[^ready_gov_landslides]
- Report broken utility lines and damaged roads to the authorities.[^ready_gov_landslides]
- Have trained professionals check the foundation, chimney and land around your home.[^ready_gov_landslides] {ref:after}
