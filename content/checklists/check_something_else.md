## Use this when

Something is wrong and no other checklist fits.

## Do first

1. **Get away from danger.** If you smell gas, see fire or smoke, or the building looks damaged, get everyone out.[^ready_gov_safety_skills][^usfa_home_fire_escape_plans][^ready_gov_recovering]
2. **Call 911** if anyone is hurt or in danger.[^dhs_report_suspicious]
3. **If a teen or adult's heart has stopped,** do hands-only CPR.[^medlineplus_cpr]
4. **Check on everyone** in your household, and ask who needs help.[^redcross_power_outage]
5. **Get official news** from phone alerts, radio or TV.[^ready_gov_alerts]
6. **Text your out-of-area contact** to say where you are.[^fcc_emergency_calling_tips]

## Then

1. **Follow instructions from local officials.**[^ready_gov_evacuation]
2. **Keep a record.** Take photos, list any damage, and keep every receipt.[^ready_gov_recovering][^fema_home_inspections] {ref:after}
3. **Talk to someone** if you need to. Call or text 988, or 1-800-985-5990 after a disaster.[^samhsa_988][^samhsa_disaster_distress]

## Leave or stay

- **Leave if** officials tell you to, or the home is unsafe. Take phones, chargers, medicines, ID and cash. Go to {where_go}.[^ready_gov_evacuation][^cdc_evacuation_psa] {ref:getting_out}
- **Stay if** officials tell you to stay inside. Go to {shelter_home}.[^ready_gov_shelter] {ref:home}
- **Go to** {meeting_near} if you are separated from your household.[^ready_gov_plan] {ref:neighbourhood}

## Where and who

- Out-of-area contact: {out_of_area_contact}
- Nearest hospital: {hospital}
- County emergency office: {county}

## Do not

- Do not go into a building that smells of gas or looks damaged.[^ready_gov_recovering]
- Never use a generator, camp stove or charcoal grill inside, even in the garage.[^cdc_co_basics][^ready_gov_power_outages]
- Stay at least 35 feet from a downed power line and anything it touches.[^redcross_power_outage]
- Never walk or drive through floodwater.[^ready_gov_floods]

## When it is over

- Apply for FEMA help at 1-800-621-3362 if your county has a federal disaster declaration.[^disasterassistance_gov] {ref:after}
