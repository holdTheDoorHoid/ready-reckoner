## Use this when

Your pharmacy cannot fill a prescription, or you hear that a medicine you take is in short supply.

## Do first

1. **Call other pharmacies.**[^fda_drug_shortages_faq]
2. **Ask your prescriber or pharmacist** whether another medicine would work.[^fda_drug_shortages_faq]
3. **Search FDA's drug shortage list** by the medicine's generic name.[^fda_drug_shortages]

## Then

1. **Ask your drug plan** about a 60- to 90-day supply once you can refill.[^medicare_drugs_disaster]
2. **Keep a written list** of each person's medicines, doses and allergies.[^ready_gov_disability]

## Leave or stay

- **Do this if** you will run out before you can refill: ask your doctor or pharmacist about an emergency supply.[^ready_gov_disability]
- **Do this if** the medicine is not on FDA's list: report the shortage to FDA.[^fda_drug_shortages]

## Where and who

- Pharmacy: {pharmacy}

## Do not

- Do not buy medicine from a website that is not a state-licensed pharmacy.[^fda_besaferx]
- Do not use expired medicines.[^fda_expired_medicines]
- Do not share antibiotics or save them for later.[^cdc_antibiotic_use]

## When it is over

- Keep at least a two-week supply of each daily prescription.[^florida_dem_medication]
- Update your written medicine list.[^ready_gov_disability]
