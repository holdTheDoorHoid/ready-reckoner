## Use this when

A volcano near you erupts or officials warn that it may, or ash is falling. Ash can make it hard to breathe, irritate your eyes and skin, and stall cars.[^ready_gov_volcanoes]{if:vei7_eruption} Ash from a big eruption can fall hundreds of miles away, so these steps apply far from the volcano too.[^ready_gov_volcanoes]{/if}

## Do first

1. **Follow orders to leave or to shelter.** If you are told to leave, go early.[^ready_gov_volcanoes]
2. **Stay out of river valleys** below the volcano and areas downwind of it.[^ready_gov_volcanoes]
3. **Get inside** and stay there while ash falls.[^ready_gov_volcanoes]
4. **Close doors and windows, and cover vents.**[^ready_gov_volcanoes]
5. **Outside?** Wear a well-fitting N95 mask. Use a cloth mask only if you have nothing better.[^ready_gov_volcanoes]
6. **Listen** for alerts and instructions.[^ready_gov_volcanoes]

## Then

1. **Limit your time outdoors.**[^ready_gov_volcanoes]
2. **Avoid driving in heavy ash.** If you have to drive, keep the windows up and the air conditioning off.[^ready_gov_volcanoes]
3. **Text** instead of calling. Save calls for emergencies.[^ready_gov_volcanoes]
4. **Breathing problems?** Stay away from the ash.[^ready_gov_volcanoes]

## Leave or stay

- **Leave if** officials tell you to. Go early.[^ready_gov_volcanoes] Go to {where_go}.{if:pets} Take your pets.[^ready_gov_pets]{/if} {ref:getting_out}
- **Stay** inside while ash falls, if you have enough supplies and are not told to leave.[^ready_gov_volcanoes] {ref:home}

## Do not

- Do not climb on the roof to clear ash unless you are trained. Ash is slippery and heavy.[^ready_gov_volcanoes]
- Do not let children help clean up.[^ready_gov_volcanoes]

## When it is over

- Go back only when officials say it is safe.[^ready_gov_volcanoes]
- Wear protective clothing and a mask to clean up.[^ready_gov_volcanoes]
- For the weeks ahead, see {ref:after}.[^ready_gov_recovering]
