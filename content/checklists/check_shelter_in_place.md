## Use this when

Officials tell you to stay inside because the outside air may be dangerous. Most orders last a few hours.[^cdc_shelter_in_place_chemical_2018]

## Do first

1. **Go inside now.** Bring everyone inside.[^cdc_shelter_in_place_chemical_2018] {if:pets}Bring pets in too.[^ready_gov_shelter]{/if}
2. **Close up.** Lock the doors. Close the windows, air vents and fireplace damper.[^ready_gov_shelter]
3. **Air off.** Turn off fans, air conditioning and forced-air heat.[^ready_gov_shelter]
4. **Pick the room.** Take your kit to an inside room with few windows.[^ready_gov_shelter]
5. **Listen.** Turn on the radio or TV for instructions.[^cdc_shelter_in_place_chemical_2018][^ready_gov_shelter] {if:need:hearing}Use a weather radio with a flashing alert and text.[^ready_gov_disability]{/if}
6. **Stay put** until officials say it is safe.[^ready_gov_chemical]

## Then

1. **Chemical spill?** Use a room above ground, because the gases sink.[^cdc_shelter_in_place_chemical_2018][^ready_gov_chemical]
2. **Radiation?** Go to a basement or the middle of the building.[^ready_gov_nuclear]
3. **Seal the room** if told to. Tape plastic sheeting over windows, doors and vents, corners first.[^ready_gov_shelter]
4. **Block gaps** under doors with wet towels.[^ready_gov_chemical]
5. **Drink stored water,** not tap water.[^cdc_shelter_in_place_chemical_2018]
6. **Save the phone** for serious emergencies.[^cdc_shelter_in_place_chemical_2018]
7. **Fumes getting in?** Take shallow breaths through a cloth or towel.[^ready_gov_chemical]

## Leave or stay

- **Stay if** officials say to shelter in place. Stay until they say it is safe.[^ready_gov_chemical] {ref:home}
- **Leave if** officials change the order to leave. Go right away.[^ready_gov_chemical] {ref:getting_out}
- **Go to** the nearest building if you are outside or in a car. Move upwind of a chemical leak.[^ready_gov_chemical]
- **Call** 911 only for a serious emergency.[^cdc_shelter_in_place_chemical_2018]

## Do not

- Do not go outside, even to help others, until officials say it is safe.[^ready_gov_chemical] {if:children}Do not go to school for your children unless told to. The school will shelter them.[^cdc_shelter_in_place_chemical_2018]{/if}
- Do not shelter in a car unless you have no other choice.[^cdc_shelter_in_place_chemical_2018]
- Do not eat or drink anything that may be contaminated.[^ready_gov_chemical]

## When it is over

- **Wait** for the all-clear on the radio or TV.[^cdc_shelter_in_place_chemical_2018]
- **Follow instructions** for going outside and making the home safe again.[^cdc_shelter_in_place_chemical_2018]
- **Exposed to a chemical?** Bag your clothes, wash with soap and water, flush your eyes, and see a doctor.[^ready_gov_chemical]
