## Use this when

Someone collapses, stops breathing normally, is choking or bleeding hard, or shows stroke or heart attack signs.

## Do first

1. **Call 911.** Give the address and do what the call-taker says.[^gov911_faq]
2. **Not breathing normally?** Push hard and fast in the center of the chest, 100 to 120 pushes a minute.[^medlineplus_cpr]
3. **Send someone for an AED.** Turn it on and follow its voice.[^medlineplus_cpr]
4. **Choking?** If they cannot cough, speak or breathe, give 5 back blows, then 5 abdominal thrusts. Repeat.[^redcross_choking_adult_child]
5. **Bleeding hard?** Press firmly on the wound with a cloth or your hands until help takes over.[^redcross_bleeding_external][^fema_until_help_arrives] {if:has:med_bleeding_control_kit}On an arm or leg, use the tourniquet if trained.[^redcross_bleeding_external]{/if}
6. **Stroke signs?** A face droops, an arm drifts down or speech is slurred. Note the time.[^cdc_stroke_signs]

## Then

1. **Heart attack signs:** chest pain or pressure, pain in the arms, jaw, neck or back, shortness of breath, feeling faint. Call 911.[^cdc_heart_attack]
2. **Keep pushing** until help arrives or they breathe normally.[^medlineplus_cpr] {if:children}Children need breaths as well as chest pushes.[^medlineplus_cpr]{/if}
3. **Choking person goes limp?** Lay them down and start CPR.[^redcross_choking_adult_child]
4. **Breathing but not awake?** Roll them onto their side.[^fema_until_help_arrives]
5. **Keep them warm.** Cover them, and put something under them.[^fema_until_help_arrives]
6. **Talk to them.** Keep them calm.[^fema_until_help_arrives]
7. **Give responders** their medicine list and allergies (People tab).[^ready_gov_disability]
8. **Cannot call?** Text 911.[^gov911_faq]

## Leave or stay

- **Stay if** it is safe there. Stay with them until help arrives.[^redcross_bleeding_external]
- **Leave if** it is not safe there. Move them out of danger first, such as out of traffic.[^fema_until_help_arrives]
- **Call** 911 for an ambulance for stroke signs. Do not drive them yourself.[^cdc_stroke_signs]

## Do not

- Do not hang up until the call-taker tells you to.[^gov911_faq]
- Do not start back blows while they can still cough hard. Encourage them to keep coughing.[^redcross_choking_adult_child]
- Do not stop pressing to put on a bandage. Pressure stops the bleeding.[^fema_until_help_arrives]

## When it is over

- Stroke signs that went away still need a doctor right away.[^cdc_stroke_signs]
- Restock the first-aid kit and replace what you used.[^redcross_first_aid_kit]
- Take a first aid and CPR class.[^ready_gov_safety_skills]
- Feeling shaken is normal. Call or text 988 to talk.[^samhsa_988]
