## Use this when

Health officials say a new disease is spreading from person to person across many countries.[^ready_gov_pandemic]

## Do first

1. **Follow health officials.** Follow CDC and your state and local health departments.[^ready_gov_pandemic]
2. **Stay home when sick,** except to get medical care. Stay away from others at home too.[^ready_gov_pandemic][^cdc_respiratory_when_sick]
3. **Wash hands** often with soap and water, for at least 20 seconds. With no soap, use hand sanitizer with at least 60 percent alcohol.[^ready_gov_pandemic]
4. **Cover coughs and sneezes.** Use a tissue.[^ready_gov_pandemic]
5. **Mask up** in public when advised. An N95 protects best.[^ready_gov_pandemic][^cdc_masks]
6. **Get vaccinated** when a vaccine is offered.[^ready_gov_pandemic]

## Then

1. **Stock up slowly.** Get food, water, cleaning supplies and prescriptions to stay home for days or weeks. Buy a little at a time.[^ready_gov_pandemic]
2. **Medicines.** Ask your doctor or pharmacist about an emergency supply.[^ready_gov_disability]
3. **Plan for closures.** Schools and workplaces may close. Plan for school and work from home.[^ready_gov_pandemic]
4. **Keep apart.** Stay 6 feet from people outside your household.[^ready_gov_pandemic]
5. **Clean** surfaces people touch often.[^ready_gov_pandemic]
6. **Exposed?** Call your doctor. Follow their quarantine advice, and watch for symptoms.[^ready_gov_pandemic]
7. **Check your insurance,** including telemedicine.[^ready_gov_pandemic]
8. **Stay in touch** by phone and video. Feeling anxious is normal. Talk to someone, or call or text 1-800-985-5990.[^ready_gov_pandemic][^samhsa_disaster_distress]

## Leave or stay

- **Stay home** as much as you can.[^ready_gov_pandemic] {ref:home}
- **Call** your doctor if you are sick or were exposed.[^ready_gov_pandemic]
- **Call** 911 in an emergency, and wear a mask while you wait.[^ready_gov_pandemic]
- **Go to** emergency care for trouble breathing or chest pain.[^cdc_respiratory_when_sick]

## Do not

- Do not go to work or school sick.[^ready_gov_pandemic]
- Do not buy everything at once.[^ready_gov_pandemic]
- Do not pass on rumors or unproven treatments.[^ready_gov_pandemic]
- Do not blame people or places for the disease.[^ready_gov_pandemic]
- Do not fall for scams.[^ready_gov_pandemic]

## When it is over

- **Keep** staying home when sick, and keep washing your hands.[^ready_gov_pandemic]
- **Update** your family emergency plan.[^ready_gov_pandemic]
- **Talk** with your community about what you learned.[^ready_gov_pandemic]
