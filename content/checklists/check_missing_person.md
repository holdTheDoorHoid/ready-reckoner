## Use this when

A child, or an adult who needs someone with them, is missing and you cannot find them.

## Do first

1. **Call 911 now.** There is no waiting period for a missing child.[^ojjdp_missing_child_guide] {if:need:cognitive|supervision}For an adult with dementia, call if a search nearby does not find them in 15 minutes, and say they have dementia.[^alz_wandering]{/if}
2. **Search the home and nearby.**[^ncmec_child_missing] {if:children}Look in closets, laundry piles, under beds, inside large appliances, and in cars and trunks.[^ncmec_child_missing]{/if}
3. **Say when you noticed** they were gone.[^ncmec_child_missing]
4. **Describe them:** height, weight, hair, eyes, glasses or braces, and what they were wearing.[^ojjdp_missing_child_guide][^ncmec_child_missing]
5. **Say where and when** they were last seen.[^ojjdp_missing_child_guide]
6. **Give police a recent photo** that looks like them on a normal day.[^ojjdp_missing_child_guide]

## Then

1. **Keep others out of the home** until police have searched it.[^ojjdp_missing_child_guide]
2. **Leave their room as it is.** Do not touch, move or remove anything.[^ojjdp_missing_child_guide]
3. **Keep their phone, computer and other devices** for police.[^ojjdp_missing_child_guide]
4. **Check what is gone,** such as clothes, a phone, money or medicines.[^ojjdp_missing_child_guide]
5. **Search where they have gone before.**[^alz_wandering] {if:need:cognitive|supervision}Check nearby water, brush and fence lines. Many people who wander are found within 1.5 miles.[^alz_wandering]{/if}
6. **Call the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children,** 1-800-843-5678, after you call police.[^ncmec_child_missing]
7. **Write down every call:** names, phone numbers, dates and times.[^ojjdp_missing_child_guide]

## Leave or stay

- **Stay if** you are at home. Keep the home as it is for police.[^ojjdp_missing_child_guide]
- **Go to** the manager or security desk first if a child goes missing in a store, then call 911.[^ncmec_child_missing]
- **Call** the police with anything new you learn.[^ojjdp_missing_child_guide]

## Do not

- Do not put off the call to do a long search yourself.[^ojjdp_missing_child_guide]
- Do not look through their phone or computer yourself.[^ojjdp_missing_child_guide]

## When it is over

- Ask police to arrange a full medical check for a found child.[^ojjdp_missing_child_guide]
- Keep a recent photo of the person to help police another time.[^nia_wandering] {if:need:cognitive|supervision}Tell neighbours and police that they tend to wander.[^nia_wandering]{/if}
- For support, call or text 988, or ask the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children.[^ojjdp_missing_child_guide]
