{frequency} It has happened: Hurricane Maria cut power to all 1.57 million electricity customers in Puerto Rico in September 2017, and it took about 11 months to restore every home.[^eia_maria_2017][^npr_maria_2018]

**About EMP.** A nuclear blast high in the sky, called an EMP, could harm electronics and black out a region. A 2019 study by the Electric Power Research Institute found little harm to large transformers. It did not support a blackout of the whole country that lasts for months or years.[^powermag_epri_2019_hemp] You may hear that an EMP would kill 9 of every 10 Americans. That claim comes from testimony to Congress about a blackout of the whole country for a year. It rests on that guess, and we found no published model behind it.[^pry_2015_emp_testimony]

**What it changes in your plan.** For very long outages, what you can do matters more than what you store: a water filter and water you can reach, a way to cook, and a way to stay warm or cool. The long-horizon part of your plan covers them when your targets reach that far.[^rr_research_risk_model]
