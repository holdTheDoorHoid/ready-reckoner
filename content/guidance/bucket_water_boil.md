{frequency} A boil-water notice means the taps still run, but germs may be in the water. Most notices come from broken water mains and repairs.[^epa_boil_water_report_2024] In Texas the most common notice lasted three to four days, and a few lasted more than a month.[^shaffer_2026_texas_boil_notices]

**What helps.** Use bottled water, or bring clear water to a rolling boil for one minute. Boil for three minutes above 5,000 feet, then let it cool.[^epa_emergency_disinfection] If you cannot boil water, add plain unscented bleach: 8 drops of 6% bleach or 6 drops of 8.25% bleach per gallon. Stir, and wait 30 minutes.[^epa_emergency_disinfection] Use boiled or bottled water for drinking, cooking, brushing teeth, baby formula and ice. If the power is out too, a camp stove works, but only outdoors.[^cdc_co_basics]

**What to avoid.** Do not use water or ice from the fridge dispenser while a notice is on. Boil tap water even if it went through a home filter or a filter pitcher.[^cdc_water_advisories] Boiling does not help if the notice warns of chemicals. That is a do-not-drink notice, and you need stored or bottled water instead.[^cdc_water_advisories][^cdc_water_disinfection]
