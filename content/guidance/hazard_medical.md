{frequency} Injuries and sudden illness are common. In 2024, accidental injuries caused about 58 deaths for every 100,000 people.[^nchs_accidental_injury_2024] Emergency departments see about 47 visits for every 100 people each year.[^cdc_nchs_ed_visits] In rural areas, ambulances take longer to arrive.[^mell_2017_ems_response]

**What helps.** Take a first-aid and bleeding-control class. You may be able to save a life by acting right after an injury, before help arrives.[^dhs_stop_the_bleed] Keep a stocked first-aid kit. Check it regularly and replace anything used or out of date.[^redcross_first_aid_kit] Keep a list of each person's medicines, doses and allergies with your emergency supplies.[^ready_gov_disability] Know how to reach 911. Call if you can, and text where that service works.[^fcc_text_911] Set up telehealth with your own clinician before you need it.[^mo_med_2026_antibiotic_kits]

**What to avoid.** Do not start antibiotics on your own or take someone else's. The wrong medicine can delay the right treatment and cause serious side effects.[^cdc_antibiotic_use] Do not use expired medicines.[^fda_expired_medicines]
