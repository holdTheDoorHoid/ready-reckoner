{frequency} Dams are rated by what a failure would do downstream, not by how likely they are to fail, so a "high hazard" dam is not necessarily unsafe.[^fema_dam_residual_risk_2018] Levees lower the risk from some floods, but they do not protect from all flooding.[^fema_living_with_levees]

**What helps.** Ask your county emergency management office whether a high-hazard dam lies upstream, and whether it has an emergency action plan.[^mo_sema_dam_failure] Ask your local government whether you live near a levee; the national levee database maps every known levee.[^fema_living_with_levees][^usace_nld] Know your evacuation route and how you would be warned.[^mo_sema_dam_failure] Behind a levee, FEMA urges flood insurance even in a moderate-risk zone, because a levee can be overtopped or fail.[^fema_nfip_levees_2021]

**What to avoid.** If a dam or levee is failing, or a flash flood is possible, get to higher ground right away.[^mo_sema_dam_failure] It is never safe to walk or drive into floodwater.[^nws_turn_around_dont_drown]
