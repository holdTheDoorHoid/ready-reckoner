{frequency} Each year, flooding kills more people than any other thunderstorm hazard. Over half of flood drownings happen when someone drives into floodwater.[^nws_turn_around_dont_drown] FEMA's high-risk flood zones are places with a 1 in 100 chance of flooding in any year.[^fema_flood_zones] Flooding does not follow lines on a map, though. Where it rains, it can flood.[^ready_gov_floods]

**What helps.** Look up your address on FEMA's flood maps, and sign up for your community's warnings.[^ready_gov_floods] Homeowner's insurance does not cover flooding, and a flood policy typically takes up to 30 days to start.[^ready_gov_floods] Renters can buy flood coverage for their belongings.[^floodsmart_buy_policy] Know how you would get to higher ground or a higher floor.[^ready_gov_floods] {if:landslide}A wildfire makes landslides more likely on a burned slope for several years. Near a burn scar, watch the forecast and listen for rushing water or a rumbling that grows louder.[^ready_gov_landslides]{/if}

**What to avoid.** Six inches of moving water can knock you down, and one foot can sweep your car away.[^ready_gov_floods] Never drive around barriers on a flooded road. The road may have collapsed under the water.[^nws_turn_around_dont_drown] Stay off bridges over fast-moving water.[^ready_gov_floods] Leave if you are told to{if:landslide}, or if you feel unsafe near a slope{/if}.[^ready_gov_landslides]
