{frequency} The chance is higher in big cities. FEMA ranks the 100 largest metro areas by their risk of terrorism when it hands out security grants.[^fema_hsgp_fy2026]

**What helps.** Keep emergency alerts turned on; they are free and need no sign-up.[^ready_gov_alerts] Agree on a meeting place, and text rather than call when networks are busy.[^ready_gov_earthquakes] If there is an incident, follow the instructions of law enforcement and listen to them for information.[^ready_gov_public_spaces] Report anything suspicious to local authorities.[^ready_gov_public_spaces]

**What to avoid.** Do not go toward the scene. If you are near it, leave in the direction the police tell you.[^ready_gov_public_spaces]
