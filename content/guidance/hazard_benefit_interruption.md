{frequency} When Congress does not pass funding in time, federal pay and some benefits can stop. In October 2025, the U.S. Department of Agriculture told every state that November SNAP benefits would not be issued. About 42 million people get SNAP.[^me_dhhs_snap_2025]

**What helps.** Keep a several-day supply of shelf-stable foods your household will eat.[^ready_gov_food] {if:benefit:snap_wic}If SNAP or WIC stops, the USDA National Hunger Hotline, 1-866-348-6479, can point you to food banks and meal sites near you.[^usda_hunger_hotline]{/if} {if:benefit:federal_pay}A furloughed federal worker may be able to claim unemployment (UCFE) in the state of their last duty station, from the first unpaid day. In most states it is repaid when back pay arrives.[^dol_ucfe_furlough_2023]{/if} If a bill will be late, call your lenders early. Regulators have urged lenders to work with people hit by a shutdown.[^cfpb_shutdown_2013]

**What to avoid.** Avoid payday loans. A typical two-week loan costs as much as borrowing at almost 400 percent a year.[^cfpb_payday_loans]
