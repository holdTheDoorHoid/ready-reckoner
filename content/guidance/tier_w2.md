Two weeks is where much official advice meets. The Red Cross suggests two weeks of supplies at home.[^redcross_survival_kit] CDC asks you to try to store two weeks of water.[^cdc_water_storage] Oregon's program is called "2 Weeks Ready."[^oregon_2_weeks_ready] Germany asks households to be able to manage for 10 days.[^bbk_vorsorgen_2025]

**Why it matters.** Many households stop before this point. In FEMA's 2024 survey, only 34 of every 100 people with supplies said theirs would last more than two weeks.[^fema_nhs_2024]

**What is in it.** More stored water, or a filter and a way to treat water. Two weeks of food, with a way to cook it outdoors if the power is out. Medicine toward a 14-day supply.[^florida_dem_medication] A two-bucket toilet, trash bags and cover material, in case toilets cannot flush.[^rdpo_emergency_toilet] A battery power station if someone relies on a medical device.[^ready_gov_disability] Build it one step at a time. Washington's guide spreads the work over 12 months.[^washington_prepare_in_a_year]

**When it is enough.** For most households, two weeks is the tier that covers every consequence target. The plan tells you when you can stop.
