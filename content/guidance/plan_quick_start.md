Learn these steps by heart. Do them in order, then turn to the checklist for what is happening.

1. **Get away from danger.** If there is fire or smoke, get everyone out and stay out.[^usfa_home_fire_escape_plans]
2. **Call 911 if anyone is hurt or in danger.** Where you cannot talk, you may be able to text 911.[^ready_gov_home_fires][^fcc_text_911]
3. **Meet and count heads.** Go to your meeting place right outside the home, or the one outside the neighborhood if you cannot get home.[^redcross_make_a_plan]
4. **Get official news.** Watch your phone for emergency alerts, and listen to a battery radio.[^ready_gov_alerts][^ready_gov_evacuation]
5. **Do what local officials say.** If they tell you to leave, leave.[^ready_gov_evacuation]
6. **Text your out-of-area contact.** Keep calls short and text when you can, so lines stay open.[^fcc_emergency_calling_tips]
