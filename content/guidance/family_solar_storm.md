{frequency} A very strong solar storm drives electric currents through long power lines and can damage the large transformers on them. A storm in March 1989 caused a nine-hour blackout in Canada.[^swpc_power_grid] Published estimates for a storm as strong as the one in 1859 run from under 1 in 100 to about 12 in 100 over ten years.[^morina_2019_carrington]

**Why here.** The effect is stronger closer to the Earth's magnetic poles, so northern states face more of it than southern ones.[^nerc_tpl007_gmd]

**Also in this family.** An asteroid strike big enough to flatten a region comes about once in thousands of years, not once in hundreds.[^nasa_tunguska_2019]

**If it reaches you.** Expect a power cut. Keep freezers and fridges closed, have a flashlight for each person, and follow your power plan.[^ready_gov_power_outages]

**What it changes in your plan.** Nothing beyond your power and water plans. The damage is to the power grid itself, which a household cannot protect.[^swpc_power_grid]
