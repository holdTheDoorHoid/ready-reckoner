A one-year tier appears only when a target is longer than six months, or when you ask for it. Items for rare disasters, such as a radiation meter, also sit here and are marked as such.[^rr_research_risk_model]

**What is in it.** Long-term food storage is built from dry staples such as wheat, white rice and beans, which can last 30 years or more when packed well and kept cool and dry.[^church_home_storage_2007] An older guide listed, for one adult for a year, 400 pounds of grains, 60 pounds of beans, 16 pounds of milk powder, oil, sugar and salt.[^ensign_2006_year_supply] If you would live on stored food for more than three months, plan for vitamins as well as calories, for example with a multivitamin or some fresh food.[^usu_food_storage_booklet] Heat shortens shelf life, so store food cool.[^usu_food_storage_booklet]

**What to avoid.** Do not let this tier crowd out the basics. The plan caps spending on rare disasters, because a tiny chance times a huge loss should not take your whole budget.[^rr_research_risk_model] Water, medicine, heat and neighbours come first. Potassium iodide is taken only on official instruction, and only near a nuclear plant.[^cdc_potassium_iodide]
