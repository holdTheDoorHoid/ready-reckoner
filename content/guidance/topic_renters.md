Renters face disasters with less cushion. In FEMA's 2024 survey, 43 of every 100 renters had insurance, compared with 87 of every 100 owners. Renters were also less likely to have money set aside.[^fema_nhs_2024] After a disaster, renters are more likely to be out of their homes for a long time. Nearly 1 in 4 displaced renter households never went back, compared with 1 in 10 owners.[^census_pulse_displacement]

**What helps.** Get renters insurance if you do not have it, and keep it up to date.[^ready_gov_financial][^fema_effak] Standard renters policies usually do not cover floods, but you can buy flood coverage for your belongings.[^floodsmart_buy_policy] Photograph your belongings and keep copies of your lease and key documents away from home.[^fema_effak] If your home has no working smoke alarms, ask your landlord or rental agent to install them.[^usfa_smoke_alarm_renters] Most preparation needs no drilling or permission: water in bottles, a go-bag, a contact card and neighbours' numbers. For power, a battery power station works indoors because it burns no fuel.[^cdc_co_basics]

**What to avoid.** Do not run a generator on a balcony or near windows. It must be outdoors, more than 20 feet from windows, doors and vents.[^cdc_co_basics] Do not assume the building has a plan. Ask where the exits, shut-offs and meeting points are.
