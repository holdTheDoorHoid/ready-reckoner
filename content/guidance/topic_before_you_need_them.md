This article draws on a 2022 talk about risk and preparedness by the security researcher Deviant Ollam. His main point: line up help on a calm day, because in a crisis there is little time and little room to think. The best help of all is a few people who have agreed ahead of time to help each other.[^ollam_2022_lawyer_passport_locksmith_gun]

**A lawyer.** Find one before you need one, and keep the number on paper.[^ollam_2022_lawyer_passport_locksmith_gun] For civil problems such as eviction, legal aid serves people with low incomes.[^lsc_get_legal_help] A will, an advance directive and powers of attorney let people you choose speak and act for you.[^nia_affairs_checklist]

**Proof of who you are.** A certified birth certificate carries the seal of the office that issued it. A passport card is a wallet-size proof of citizenship and identity that TSA accepts on domestic flights.[^state_dept_passport_card] Children can have a passport book or card too.[^state_dept_child_passport]

**A locksmith.** Look for a local locksmith before you are locked out, and save the number.[^ftc_2008_locksmith]

**Files and accounts.** Keep three copies of important files, on two kinds of storage, with one away from home.[^cisa_data_backup_2012] Use a password manager and two ways to sign in.[^ready_gov_cybersecurity]

**A skill.** A Stop the Bleed class teaches people to control serious bleeding, since bystanders reach an injured person before help does.[^dhs_stop_the_bleed]

**Test it.** A spare key nobody has tried, or a number nobody has called, may fail when you need it, so try each one once.[^ollam_2022_lawyer_passport_locksmith_gun]
