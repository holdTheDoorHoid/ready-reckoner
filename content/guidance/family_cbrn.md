{frequency} These attacks are rare. A university database of such incidents counts 517 worldwide from 1990 to 2016, and that includes failed attempts and plots.[^start_poicn]

**If it reaches you.** For a chemical release, go into an inside room above ground, close windows, doors and vents, and seal the room if told to.[^ready_gov_chemical] For a biological attack, follow the instructions of doctors and public health officials, and avoid crowds.[^ready_gov_biohazard] For a radiation emergency, get inside, stay inside and stay tuned.[^ready_gov_radiation]

**What it changes in your plan.** Nothing beyond what you already keep to stay inside: plastic sheeting and duct tape to seal a room, and water and food for several days.[^ready_gov_chemical][^ready_gov_kit]
