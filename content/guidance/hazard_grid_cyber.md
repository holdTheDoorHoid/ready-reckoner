{frequency} Not every outage comes from the weather. Equipment failures and cyberattacks can take down the power grid, card payments, or pharmacy and insurance systems for days.[^rr_research_risk_model] Closer to home, a broken water main can bring a boil-water notice. Most notices come from main breaks and repairs.[^epa_boil_water_report_2024]

**What helps.** The same basic supplies cover all of these: water, food, lights, a radio and a backup phone battery.[^ready_gov_kit] Keep some cash in small bills, because ATMs and credit cards may not work.[^ready_gov_financial] Write important phone numbers on paper, not only in your phone.[^ready_gov_low_cost] Keep a written list of each person's medicines and doses.[^ready_gov_disability] Protect your accounts with a password manager and two-step login, and back up your files regularly.[^ready_gov_cybersecurity] Install software updates without delay.[^cisa_secure_our_world]

**What to avoid.** Do not click links in texts or emails from people you don't know. Scammers can make fake websites.[^ready_gov_cybersecurity] Learn to spot phishing: messages that try to get you to open an attachment or share personal information.[^cisa_secure_our_world] During a boil-water notice, do not use water or ice from the fridge dispenser.[^cdc_water_advisories]
