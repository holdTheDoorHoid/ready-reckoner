{frequency} Earthquakes strike without warning. Afterward, gas and water lines may leak, and aftershocks can follow.[^ready_gov_earthquakes] {if:tsunami}On the coast, a tsunami can follow a big earthquake within minutes.[^dogami_tsunami_faq]{/if}

**What helps.** Practice Drop, Cover and Hold On: drop to your hands and knees, cover your head and neck, and hold on until the shaking stops.[^ready_gov_earthquakes] Secure bookcases, water heaters, TVs and other heavy things, and keep heavy objects on low shelves. A standard homeowner's policy does not cover earthquake damage.[^ready_gov_earthquakes] Store water in case the taps stop. {if:tsunami}On the coast, the shaking is your warning. As soon as it stops, walk to high ground or inland without waiting for an alert.[^dogami_tsunami_faq] A loud roar from the ocean, or water that suddenly drains away, is another sign.[^ready_gov_tsunamis]{/if} {if:volcanic_activity}Near a volcano, leave early if you are told to. Ash can travel hundreds of miles, so keep N95 masks.[^ready_gov_volcanoes]{/if}

**What to avoid.** Do not run outside during shaking, and stay away from doorways.[^ready_gov_earthquakes] {if:tsunami}Do not go back to the shore until officials say it is safe. Tsunami waves can keep coming for hours.[^dogami_tsunami_faq]{/if} {if:volcanic_activity}Avoid driving in heavy ash.[^ready_gov_volcanoes]{/if}
