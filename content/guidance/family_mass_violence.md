{frequency} In 2024 the FBI counted 24 attacks across the whole country in which someone was actively trying to kill people in a public place.[^fbi_active_shooter_2024] Being hurt in one is very rare.

**If it happens near you.** Getting away is the top priority: leave your belongings and run. If you cannot, hide out of sight behind something solid. Fight only as a last resort. When it is safe, call 911 and help the wounded.[^ready_gov_public_spaces] A person who is bleeding badly can die within five minutes, so what bystanders do first matters.[^dhs_stop_the_bleed]

**What it changes in your plan.** Nothing to buy. Two free steps help: know run, hide, fight, and take a Stop the Bleed class.[^ready_gov_public_spaces][^dhs_stop_the_bleed]
