{frequency} A study of the last 400 years of outbreaks put the chance of a pandemic as bad as COVID-19 at about 2 in 100 in any year. For one as deadly as the 1918 flu, it put the chance at 3 to 19 in 1,000 a year.[^duke_2021_pandemics] The 1918 pandemic killed about 675,000 people in the United States.[^cdc_pandemic_history]

**If it reaches you.** Stay home when you are sick, except to get medical care, wash your hands often, and follow public health guidance.[^ready_gov_pandemic]

**What it changes in your plan.** Nothing new. Ready.gov suggests supplies for staying home for several days or weeks, such as food, prescriptions and water, and your plan already sizes those.[^ready_gov_pandemic]
