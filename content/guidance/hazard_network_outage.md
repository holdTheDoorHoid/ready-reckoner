{frequency} A phone network can go down across the country. In February 2024, one national carrier's outage lasted at least 12 hours, blocked more than 92 million calls and stopped more than 25,000 calls to 911.[^fcc_att_outage_2024]

**What helps.** Write the regular phone numbers of your police and fire departments next to 911 on your family card.[^ready_gov_family_comm_card] Turn on Wi-Fi calling now. In that outage the carrier told customers to use it, and some could not switch it on once the network was down.[^fcc_att_outage_2024] Agree where your household will meet if you cannot reach each other.[^ready_gov_earthquakes]

**What to avoid.** Do not keep important numbers only in your phone.[^ready_gov_low_cost]
