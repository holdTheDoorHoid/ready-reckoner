{frequency} Most outages last a few hours. In 2024 the average home lost power for about 11 hours in total, mostly because of hurricanes, and a big storm can cut power for days.[^eia_outage_hours_2024]

**What helps.** Give each person a headlamp and each phone a charged power bank, and keep a battery or crank radio. Keep the fridge and freezer closed. A closed fridge keeps food cold for about four hours, and a full freezer for about two days.[^ready_gov_food][^ready_gov_power_outages] If someone relies on a powered medical device, set up backup power now. Ask your power company to put you on its list for priority restoration.[^ready_gov_disability] A small battery power station is safe to use indoors because it burns no fuel.

**What to avoid.** Never run a generator, grill or camp stove inside your home or garage, even with the doors open. Carbon monoxide has no smell and can kill.[^cdc_co_basics] Put a carbon monoxide alarm on every level. Use flashlights, not candles. Unplug electronics so a power surge does not damage them when the power returns.
