{frequency} Police reported about 6.1 million crashes in 2023.[^nhtsa_crashes_2023] Breakdowns, closed roads and storms can also leave you stuck for hours.

**What helps.** Keep a car kit with jumper cables, sand, a flashlight, warm clothes, blankets, water and snacks.[^ready_gov_winter] Keep the gas tank at least half full.[^ready_gov_evacuation] Check the pressure in all your tires, including the spare, at least once a month.[^nhtsa_tires] Before a winter trip, tell someone where you are going and when you expect to arrive, and ask them to call for help if you are late.[^cdc_winter_safety] If you drive an electric car, know its range and where you can charge along your route.[^hcfl_ev_safety][^doe_afdc_stations]

**What to avoid.** If you are stuck in a snowstorm, staying in the car is often safest. Tie a bright cloth to the antenna. Run the engine about 10 minutes an hour with a window open a little, and keep the exhaust pipe clear of snow.[^cdc_winter_safety] Never drive into a flooded road or around the barriers.[^nws_turn_around_dont_drown]
