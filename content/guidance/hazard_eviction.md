{frequency} From 2000 to 2016, landlords filed about 1 eviction case each year for every 17 renter households, and about 1 in 40 renter households were evicted each year.[^eviction_lab_national]

**What helps.** If rent is getting hard to pay, ask for help early. Call 211 or your local government about rental assistance, which can take several weeks to arrive.[^cfpb_rent_help] Find the free legal aid office near you now.[^lsc_get_legal_help] If a landlord sues, you have the right to file a written answer and to tell the court your side. Call the court clerk to ask the deadline, the court date, and whether mediation is offered.[^cfpb_facing_eviction]

**What to avoid.** Do not ignore court papers. Too many renters give up before they have a chance to go to court.[^cfpb_facing_eviction]
