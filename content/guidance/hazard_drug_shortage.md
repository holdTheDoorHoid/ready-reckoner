{frequency} Medicines can run short when makers have quality problems or delays, or stop making a drug. Often an empty shelf is a short, local problem, and more is on the way.[^fda_drug_shortages]

**What helps.** Keep at least a two-week supply of each daily prescription.[^florida_dem_medication] If your pharmacy is out, try other pharmacies, and ask your prescriber or pharmacist whether another medicine would work.[^fda_drug_shortages_faq] You can search FDA's drug shortage list by the drug's generic name. If it is listed, ask about other treatments. If it is not, you can report it to FDA.[^fda_drug_shortages]

**What to avoid.** If you buy medicine online, use a state-licensed pharmacy. Some websites look like pharmacies but may be unsafe.[^fda_besaferx]
