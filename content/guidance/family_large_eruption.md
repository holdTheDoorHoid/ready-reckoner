{frequency} Researchers put the chance of a magnitude 7 eruption somewhere in the world at about 1 in 6 over the next 100 years. The last one, in Indonesia in 1815, cooled the whole planet by about a degree, and crops failed in what became known as the year without a summer.[^cassidy_mani_2022] A super-eruption at Yellowstone is far less likely, about 1 in 730,000 in any year.[^usgs_yvo]

**If it reaches you.** Far from the volcano, the main effect would come through the food supply, as in 1815, when crops failed.[^cassidy_mani_2022]

**What it changes in your plan.** Nothing beyond a pantry of foods your household eats.[^ready_gov_food]
