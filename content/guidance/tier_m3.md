Three months is mostly about money, not supplies. A spell out of work often lasts months. In August 2026, about 27 of every 100 unemployed people had been looking for 27 weeks or more.[^bls_unemployment_duration] For most households, income is the only need that runs to months.[^rr_research_risk_model]

**What is in it.** An emergency fund is the heart of this tier. Planners often suggest three to six months of living expenses, and more if your income varies.[^finra_financial_foundations] Start small and save automatically.[^cfpb_emergency_fund] A three-month supply of the foods you normally eat, built up a little at a time and rotated, also helps.[^church_home_storage_2007] It stretches your money in a hard month as well as in a disaster.

**When it is enough.** Few households get here. In FEMA's 2024 survey, 8 of every 100 people with supplies said theirs would last more than three months.[^fema_nhs_2024] The plan shows this tier only when one of your targets needs it, and it tracks savings separately from supplies.
