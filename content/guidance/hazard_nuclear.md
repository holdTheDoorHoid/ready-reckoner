{frequency} These events are very rare. In a 2024 survey, experts and forecasters put the chance of a nuclear catastrophe anywhere in the world before 2045 at 1 to 5 in 100.[^fri_nuclear_risk_2024] The right response is simple, and your three-day supplies already cover it.

**What helps.** Get inside, stay inside, stay tuned.[^ready_gov_nuclear] Go to a basement or the middle of a large brick or concrete building. Stay there for 24 hours unless there is a fire or another immediate danger, or officials say it is safe to leave. Radiation levels drop fast during the first 24 hours.[^ready_gov_nuclear] Turn off fans and air conditioning that pull in outside air.[^ready_gov_radiation] If you were outside, take off your outer layer of clothing. That can remove up to 90% of radioactive material. Then wash with soap and water.[^ready_gov_radiation] If you live near a nuclear power plant, learn whether you are in its planning zone.[^fema_nuclear_sites]

**What to avoid.** Do not take potassium iodide unless public health or emergency officials tell you to. It protects only the thyroid.[^cdc_potassium_iodide] Near a plant, states can give out tablets to people within 10 miles, on official instruction.[^nrc_potassium_iodide] Do not spend on special gear before your basic supplies are in place.[^rr_research_risk_model]
