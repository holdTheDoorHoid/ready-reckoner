No major U.S. authority sets a one-month household standard. Ready Reckoner uses one month as a step between two weeks and three months, and says so.[^rr_research_supply_standards] It enters your plan only when one of your targets is longer than two weeks.

**Why it can matter.** Some places face events where help and repairs take longer. Some households also face longer gaps in medicine or income. CDC suggests asking your health care provider whether you can get a 30-day emergency refill.[^cdc_pregnancy_emergency] Fewer than 2 in 10 people with supplies said theirs would last more than a month.[^fema_nhs_2024]

**What is in it.** At this length, skills and tools beat piles of supplies. A water filter and a source of raw water go further than more jugs.[^rr_research_risk_model] Food is best built from what you already eat, rotated as you go.[^fsis_shelf_stable] Freeze-dried kits often hold fewer calories than their "days" label suggests.[^rr_research_supply_standards] An emergency fund starts to matter as much as supplies.[^cfpb_emergency_fund]

**When it is enough.** When each target that is longer than two weeks is covered, the plan stops here or moves on.
