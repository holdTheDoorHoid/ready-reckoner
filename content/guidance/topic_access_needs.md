Emergency planners sort the needs a disaster makes harder under five headings, called CMIST: communication, maintaining health, independence, support and safety, and transportation.[^aspr_cmist] In FEMA's 2024 survey, 16 of every 100 people said needing help because of health or a disability kept them from preparing.[^fema_nhs_2024]

**Communication.** If speech, hearing or understanding is affected, carry printed cards that tell responders how to communicate with you.[^ready_gov_disability] {if:need:hearing}For someone who is deaf or hard of hearing, add smoke alarms with a vibrating pad or a strobe light.[^usfa_fire_safety_disabilities]{/if} {if:need:limited_english}Ready.gov has guides in Spanish at listo.gov, and in 11 other languages.[^ready_gov_your_language]{/if}

**Maintaining health.** Ask your doctor about an emergency supply of medicine, and plan backup power for equipment.[^ready_gov_disability] A power company's medical program can bring outage warnings, but it does not guarantee faster repairs.[^psegli_critical_care] {if:need:dialysis}Know more than one place that can give your treatment.[^ready_gov_disability]{/if} {if:need:home_health}Home health agencies in Medicare must write an emergency plan for each patient, so ask for your copy.[^aspr_tracie_hha_rule]{/if}

**Independence.** People stay independent when they are not separated from their mobility aids, devices and service animals, so keep them with you.[^aspr_cmist] {if:need:service_animal}Some shelters take only service or support animals. Pack food, water and supplies for yours.[^ready_gov_disability]{/if}

**Support and safety.** Make sure at least one person in your support network has a key to your home and knows how to use your equipment.[^ready_gov_older_adults] {if:need:cognitive|supervision}A strange place can be hard for someone with memory or thinking problems. Plan who will stay with them if their usual caregiver cannot.[^aspr_cmist]{/if} Many city and county emergency offices keep voluntary registries for people who may need help, and your state's entry in this plan shows where to start.[^ready_gov_disability] Signing up does not guarantee help, so keep your own plan too.[^tdem_stear]

**Transportation.** Arrange accessible transportation ahead of time with your local transit provider or emergency management office.[^ready_gov_disability]
