{frequency} A new disease can spread around the world quickly, and no one can predict when.[^ready_gov_pandemic] One study estimates that a pandemic as intense as COVID-19 comes about once every 200 years on average, which makes about a 38 in 100 chance of seeing one in a lifetime.[^marani_2021_pandemics] In spring 2020, statewide stay-at-home orders lasted about six weeks in the typical state.[^cdc_mmwr_stay_at_home_2020][^rr_research_risk_model]

**What helps.** Gather supplies in case you need to stay home for several days or weeks: food, cleaning supplies, prescriptions and water. Buy them slowly, so everyone has a chance to get what they need.[^ready_gov_pandemic] Plan how you would work, go to school and keep in touch from home.[^ready_gov_pandemic] Keep N95 masks. They protect better than cloth or surgical masks.[^cdc_masks] An emergency fund helps, because a pandemic can cut income.[^cfpb_emergency_fund] Wash your hands often with soap and water for at least 20 seconds.[^ready_gov_pandemic]

**What to avoid.** Stay home when you are sick, except to get medical care.[^ready_gov_pandemic] Do not rush to buy everything at once. Watch out for scams and fraud.[^ready_gov_pandemic]
