{frequency} Burst pipes, leaks and freezing are among the most common home insurance claims. Insured homes file them about six times as often as fire claims.[^iii_homeowners_losses]

**What helps.** Find your main water shut-off valve now, and show everyone how to turn it off. A burst pipe can flood a room quickly.[^iii_water_damage_protect][^nws_aly_cold_safety] Once a year, check the hoses to the water heater, washing machine, dishwasher and fridge, and replace any that crack or leak. A leak alarm can warn you early.[^iii_water_damage_protect] In freezing weather, let a faucet drip at night, open the cabinet doors under sinks on outside walls, and keep the heat at 55°F or higher.[^nws_aly_cold_safety] Home and renters policies usually cover burst pipes. Many do not cover a sewer backup unless you add that cover, and floods need their own policy.[^iii_water_damage_protect]

**What to avoid.** Never thaw a frozen pipe with an open flame; use a hair dryer. Watch for electric shock around standing water.[^nws_aly_cold_safety] Do not leave home while the washing machine or dishwasher is running.[^iii_water_damage_protect]
