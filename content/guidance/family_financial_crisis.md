{frequency} In March 1933, the president declared a nationwide bank holiday, closing banks across the country. Congress created federal deposit insurance that June.[^fdic_history_1930s] Today, deposits are insured to at least $250,000 at each FDIC-insured bank.[^fdic_deposit_insurance]

**If it reaches you.** In 1933 the banks began to reopen after a week.[^fdic_history_1930s] A closure like that would make it hard to reach your money for days, even when your deposits are insured.[^fdic_deposit_insurance]

**What it changes in your plan.** Keep some cash at home.[^ready_gov_kit] Deposit insurance counts separately at each insured bank, so an account at a second bank gives you another place to keep money.[^fdic_deposit_insurance]
