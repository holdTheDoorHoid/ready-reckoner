{frequency} In a 2024 survey, experts put the chance of a deadly military clash between Russia and the United States before 2030 at about 5 in 100, and between China and the United States at about 10 in 100. Expert forecasters put both lower.[^fri_nuclear_risk_2024]

**If it reaches you.** US agencies have warned that hackers who work for China's government got into American phone, power, transport and water systems. The aim was to be ready to shut them down in a war.[^cisa_volt_typhoon_2024] So at home, the likely effects are outages of the kind your plan already covers.

**What it changes in your plan.** Nothing beyond your basics: water, food, a battery or hand-crank radio, some cash, and copies of important documents.[^ready_gov_kit] Keep key phone numbers somewhere besides your phone.[^ready_gov_low_cost]
