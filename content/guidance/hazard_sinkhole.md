{frequency} Sinkholes form where the rock underground, such as limestone, can be dissolved by water. The most damage from them happens in Florida, Texas, Alabama, Missouri, Kentucky, Tennessee and Pennsylvania.[^usgs_sinkholes]

**What helps.** If a hole or sinking ground threatens your home, call your insurance company right away. Only a licensed geologist or engineer can tell a true sinkhole from other causes of sinking ground. Keep people and pets away from a new hole by fencing or marking it off.[^fl_dep_sinkhole_faq]

**What to avoid.** If lives or your home are in immediate danger, call 911, and follow local officials' advice about leaving. Stay back from the edge: the sides can keep slumping for about a day.[^fl_dep_sinkhole_faq]
